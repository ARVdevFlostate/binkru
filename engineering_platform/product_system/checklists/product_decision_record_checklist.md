# Product Decision Record Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist assists reviewers in evaluating whether a Product Decision Record conforms to the Product System governance, Product Decision Record Methodology and Product Decision Record Template.

It is intended to support consistent review prior to approval.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all Product Decision Record artifacts governed by the Product System.

---

# 3. Review Checklist

## Artifact Identity

- [ ] An Artifact ID has been assigned.
- [ ] A Decision ID has been assigned.
- [ ] Product information has been completed.
- [ ] Version information is present.
- [ ] Ownership is identified.
- [ ] Approval information has been completed or is ready for approval.

---

## Decision Summary

- [ ] The Decision Summary is clearly stated.
- [ ] The summary accurately reflects the decision.
- [ ] The summary avoids implementation details.

---

## Decision Context

- [ ] The decision context is clearly described.
- [ ] The context provides sufficient background.
- [ ] The context supports understanding of the decision.

---

## Decision

- [ ] The product decision is clearly stated.
- [ ] The decision is unambiguous.
- [ ] The decision aligns with the Product Vision.

---

## Decision Rationale

- [ ] The rationale clearly explains why the decision was made.
- [ ] The rationale supports the selected decision.
- [ ] The rationale is objective and evidence-based.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- [ ] Significant alternatives have been documented where applicable.
- [ ] Alternatives have been considered fairly.
- [ ] The reasons for not selecting alternatives are documented where appropriate.

---

## Expected Outcomes

- [ ] Expected outcomes are clearly described.
- [ ] Outcomes support the Product Vision.
- [ ] Outcomes focus on product value rather than implementation activities.

---

## Impacts

- [ ] Significant impacts have been identified.
- [ ] Impacts appropriately consider customer, business, operational, technical and regulatory perspectives.
- [ ] Impacts are consistent with the decision.

---

## Assumptions

- [ ] Significant assumptions have been documented.
- [ ] Assumptions are relevant to the decision.

---

## Constraints

- [ ] Significant constraints have been documented.
- [ ] Constraints appropriately reflect the decision context.

---

## Related Artifacts

- [ ] The Product Vision has been referenced.
- [ ] Related Product Decision Records have been referenced where applicable.
- [ ] Downstream artifacts have been identified where appropriate.
- [ ] References are accurate.

---

## Governance

- [ ] The decision is traceable to the Product Vision.
- [ ] The decision is consistent with previous Product Decision Records.
- [ ] The decision supports subsequent product artifacts.
- [ ] The Product Decision Record has undergone appropriate review.
- [ ] The Product Decision Record is ready for Product Owner approval.
