# Milestone Plan Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist assists reviewers in evaluating whether a Milestone Plan conforms to the Product System governance, Milestone Plan Methodology and Milestone Plan Template.

It is intended to support consistent review prior to approval.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all Milestone Plan artifacts governed by the Product System.

---

# 3. Review Checklist

## Artifact Identity

- [ ] An Artifact ID has been assigned.
- [ ] A Milestone Plan ID has been assigned.
- [ ] Product information has been completed.
- [ ] Version information is present.
- [ ] Ownership is identified.
- [ ] Approval information has been completed or is ready for approval.

---

## Milestone Plan Summary

- [ ] The Milestone Plan Summary is clearly stated.
- [ ] The summary accurately reflects the intended progression of measurable product outcomes.
- [ ] The summary avoids delivery scope and implementation details.

---

## Strategic Context

- [ ] The strategic context is clearly described.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan aligns with the Product Vision.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan reflects approved Product Decision Records.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan aligns with the Product Roadmap.

---

## Milestones

- [ ] Milestones are clearly defined.
- [ ] Each milestone supports one or more strategic initiatives.
- [ ] Each milestone represents a measurable product outcome.
- [ ] Success criteria have been defined for each milestone.
- [ ] Dependencies have been identified where applicable.
- [ ] Target timeframes are appropriate where provided.

---

## Milestone Sequencing

- [ ] The sequencing rationale is clearly documented.
- [ ] Milestone ordering supports strategic product evolution.
- [ ] Milestone sequencing reflects logical progression.
- [ ] Sequencing considers identified dependencies.

---

## Assumptions

- [ ] Significant assumptions have been documented.
- [ ] Assumptions are relevant to the Milestone Plan.

---

## Constraints

- [ ] Significant constraints have been documented.
- [ ] Constraints appropriately reflect the planning context.

---

## Risks

- [ ] Significant milestone risks have been identified.
- [ ] Risks appropriately reflect product outcome planning.
- [ ] Risks are consistent with the Milestone Plan.

---

## Success Characteristics

- [ ] Success Characteristics are clearly described.
- [ ] Success Characteristics support the Product Vision.
- [ ] Success Characteristics focus on measurable product outcomes rather than delivery activities.

---

## Related Artifacts

- [ ] The Product Vision has been referenced.
- [ ] Relevant Product Decision Records have been referenced.
- [ ] The Product Roadmap has been referenced.
- [ ] Downstream planning artifacts have been identified where appropriate.
- [ ] References are accurate.

---

## Governance

- [ ] The Milestone Plan is traceable to the Product Vision.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan is traceable to approved Product Decision Records.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan is traceable to the Product Roadmap.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan communicates measurable product outcomes rather than delivery scope.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan supports subsequent Release Planning.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan has undergone appropriate review.
- [ ] The Milestone Plan is ready for Product Owner approval.
