# Product Vision Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist assists reviewers in evaluating whether a Product Vision conforms to the Product System governance, Vision Methodology and Vision Template.

It is intended to support consistent review prior to approval.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all Product Vision artifacts governed by the Product System.

---

# 3. Review Checklist

## Artifact Identity

- [ ] An Artifact ID has been assigned.
- [ ] Product information has been completed.
- [ ] Version information is present.
- [ ] Ownership is identified.
- [ ] Approval information has been completed or is ready for approval.

---

## Product Purpose

- [ ] The Product Purpose is clearly stated.
- [ ] The purpose explains why the product exists.
- [ ] The purpose avoids implementation details.

---

## Product Direction

- [ ] The long-term direction is clearly described.
- [ ] The direction supports the Product Purpose.
- [ ] The direction remains implementation independent.

---

## Customer Value

- [ ] Intended customer value is clearly identified.
- [ ] Customer value aligns with the Product Purpose.
- [ ] Customer value is expressed in terms of outcomes.

---

## Product Principles

- [ ] Product Principles are defined.
- [ ] Principles support long-term product evolution.
- [ ] Principles are stable rather than tactical.

---

## Success Characteristics

- [ ] Success Characteristics describe desired outcomes.
- [ ] Success Characteristics support the Product Direction.
- [ ] Success Characteristics avoid implementation milestones.

---

## Assumptions

- [ ] Significant assumptions have been documented.
- [ ] Assumptions are relevant to the Product Vision.

---

## Constraints

- [ ] Significant constraints have been documented.
- [ ] Constraints appropriately reflect product context.

---

## Related Artifacts

- [ ] Related artifacts have been identified where applicable.
- [ ] References are accurate.

---

## Governance

- [ ] The Vision aligns with Product Principles.
- [ ] The Vision is internally consistent.
- [ ] The Vision supports subsequent product artifacts.
- [ ] The Vision has undergone appropriate review.
- [ ] The Vision is ready for Product Owner approval.

---
