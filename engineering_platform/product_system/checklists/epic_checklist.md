# Epic Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist validates that a draft Epic satisfies the requirements of the Epic Methodology before it enters the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement.

The checklist helps ensure that Product has completed its responsibilities, preserved approved business intent and established sufficient quality for governed collaboration with Engineering.

---

# 1. Business Alignment

- [ ] The Epic is derived from an approved Release Plan.
- [ ] The Epic represents a coherent Product Capability.
- [ ] The Epic supports the intended business objectives.
- [ ] The Epic preserves approved business intent.
- [ ] The Epic aligns with Product priorities.

---

# 2. Business Scope

- [ ] Business scope has been clearly defined.
- [ ] Included scope has been identified.
- [ ] Excluded scope has been identified.
- [ ] Business assumptions have been documented.
- [ ] Business constraints have been documented.

---

# 3. Expected Outcomes

- [ ] Expected business outcomes have been defined.
- [ ] Customer outcomes have been identified.
- [ ] Success criteria have been documented.
- [ ] Business value has been clearly articulated.

---

# 4. Dependencies and Risks

- [ ] Business dependencies have been identified.
- [ ] External dependencies have been documented.
- [ ] Business risks have been identified.
- [ ] Open questions have been recorded.

---

# 5. Traceability

- [ ] Traceability to the Product Vision has been established.
- [ ] Traceability to Product Decision Records has been established.
- [ ] Traceability to the Product Roadmap has been established.
- [ ] Traceability to the Milestone Plan has been established.
- [ ] Traceability to the Release Plan has been established.

---

# 6. Product Review

- [ ] Product review has been completed.
- [ ] Review observations have been recorded.
- [ ] Outstanding concerns have been addressed or recorded.
- [ ] Product approval has been obtained.

---

# 7. Readiness

- [ ] The Epic is approved as a Product draft Epic.
- [ ] The Epic is ready to enter the Collaboration System.
- [ ] The Epic contains sufficient business context for collaborative refinement.
- [ ] Required Product artifacts are complete.
- [ ] Required supporting information is available.

---

# 8. Governance

- [ ] The Epic conforms to the Epic Methodology.
- [ ] The Epic Template has been completed.
- [ ] Required Product responsibilities have been fulfilled.
- [ ] Human accountability has been established.
- [ ] AI contributions (if any) have been appropriately reviewed.

---

# Completion

The Epic Checklist is complete when all mandatory checklist items have been satisfied or appropriate exceptions have been documented.

Successful completion confirms that the Product System has fulfilled its responsibilities and that the draft Epic is ready to enter the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement.
