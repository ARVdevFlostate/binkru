# Product Roadmap Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist assists reviewers in evaluating whether a Product Roadmap conforms to the Product System governance, Roadmap Methodology and Roadmap Template.

It is intended to support consistent review prior to approval.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all Product Roadmap artifacts governed by the Product System.

---

# 3. Review Checklist

## Artifact Identity

- [ ] An Artifact ID has been assigned.
- [ ] A Roadmap ID has been assigned.
- [ ] Product information has been completed.
- [ ] Version information is present.
- [ ] Ownership is identified.
- [ ] Approval information has been completed or is ready for approval.

---

## Roadmap Summary

- [ ] The Roadmap Summary is clearly stated.
- [ ] The summary accurately reflects the strategic direction of the roadmap.
- [ ] The summary avoids implementation details.

---

## Strategic Context

- [ ] The strategic context is clearly described.
- [ ] The roadmap aligns with the Product Vision.
- [ ] The roadmap reflects approved Product Decision Records.

---

## Strategic Initiatives

- [ ] Strategic initiatives are clearly defined.
- [ ] Each initiative supports the Product Vision.
- [ ] Each initiative is traceable to one or more approved Product Decision Records.
- [ ] Initiative priorities are appropriate.
- [ ] Dependencies have been identified where applicable.
- [ ] Target timeframes are appropriate where provided.

---

## Initiative Prioritisation

- [ ] The prioritisation rationale is clearly documented.
- [ ] Initiative ordering supports strategic objectives.
- [ ] Prioritisation reflects customer and business value.

---

## Assumptions

- [ ] Significant assumptions have been documented.
- [ ] Assumptions are relevant to the roadmap.

---

## Constraints

- [ ] Significant constraints have been documented.
- [ ] Constraints appropriately reflect the roadmap context.

---

## Risks

- [ ] Significant roadmap risks have been identified.
- [ ] Risks appropriately reflect strategic planning.
- [ ] Risks are consistent with the roadmap.

---

## Success Characteristics

- [ ] Success Characteristics are clearly described.
- [ ] Success Characteristics support the Product Vision.
- [ ] Success Characteristics focus on strategic outcomes rather than delivery milestones.

---

## Related Artifacts

- [ ] The Product Vision has been referenced.
- [ ] Relevant Product Decision Records have been referenced.
- [ ] Downstream planning artifacts have been identified where appropriate.
- [ ] References are accurate.

---

## Governance

- [ ] The roadmap is traceable to the Product Vision.
- [ ] The roadmap is traceable to approved Product Decision Records.
- [ ] The roadmap provides strategic direction rather than delivery commitments.
- [ ] The roadmap supports subsequent planning artifacts.
- [ ] The Product Roadmap has undergone appropriate review.
- [ ] The Product Roadmap is ready for Product Owner approval.

---
