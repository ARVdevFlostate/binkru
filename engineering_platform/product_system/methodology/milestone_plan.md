# Milestone Plan Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This methodology defines the recommended approach for developing, reviewing and maintaining a Milestone Plan within the Product System.

A Milestone Plan translates strategic initiatives into measurable product outcomes by defining significant milestones that demonstrate meaningful progress toward the Product Vision. It provides a structured approach for planning major product outcomes while preserving flexibility as implementation evolves.

This methodology describes the process for creating a Milestone Plan. It does not define the structure of the artifact, which is specified by the Milestone Plan Template.

---

# 2. Scope

This methodology applies to the creation, review and evolution of Milestone Plans governed by the Product System.

It is intended for:

- Product Owners
- Product Managers
- Product Leadership
- Product Contributors
- AI assistants supporting product development

---

# 3. Principles

A Milestone Plan should:

- translate strategic initiatives into measurable product outcomes;
- define meaningful progress toward the Product Vision;
- remain outcome-oriented rather than implementation-oriented;
- organise milestones into a coherent progression;
- support subsequent Release Planning; and
- remain adaptable as product understanding evolves.

---

# 4. Responsibilities

The Product Owner is responsible for:

- defining the Milestone Plan;
- ensuring alignment with the Product Vision and Product Roadmap;
- approving milestone revisions; and
- maintaining the Milestone Plan throughout the product lifecycle.

Contributors may participate in milestone definition, sequencing and planning.

AI may assist with milestone analysis, sequencing, dependency analysis and impact assessment but does not determine milestone priorities or approve the Milestone Plan.

---

# 5. Inputs

A Milestone Plan may be developed using information available at the time of creation.

## Required Inputs

The following inputs are required:

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Records
- Product Roadmap

The Milestone Plan should reflect the approved strategic direction and roadmap initiatives.

## Optional Inputs

Where available, the following inputs may assist in developing a Milestone Plan:

- business objectives;
- customer research;
- stakeholder feedback;
- market analysis;
- organisational priorities;
- product constraints;
- lessons learned;
- previous Milestone Plans; and
- strategic dependencies.

The relevance and availability of these inputs depend on the product context.

---

# 6. Method

A Milestone Plan should be developed through an iterative process.

Typical activities include:

1. Review the Product Vision.
2. Review approved Product Decision Records.
3. Review the Product Roadmap.
4. Identify measurable product outcomes.
5. Define significant milestones.
6. Determine milestone sequencing.
7. Identify milestone dependencies.
8. Review milestone alignment.
9. Refine the Milestone Plan based on feedback.
10. Approve the Milestone Plan.

The methodology encourages outcome-based planning rather than implementation-based planning.

---

# 7. Review and Approval

A Milestone Plan should be reviewed for:

- alignment with the Product Vision;
- consistency with approved Product Decision Records;
- alignment with the Product Roadmap;
- clarity of milestones;
- measurable outcomes;
- completeness;
- adaptability; and
- governance compliance.

The approved Milestone Plan becomes the authoritative product outcome planning artifact.

---

# 8. Evolution

A Milestone Plan is expected to evolve throughout the product lifecycle.

Changes should reflect approved Product Decisions, evolving roadmap priorities and improved product understanding.

Superseded Milestone Plan versions remain part of the product history.

---

# 9. Relationship to Other Artifacts

A Milestone Plan derives from the Product Vision, approved Product Decision Records and the Product Roadmap.

Subsequent product artifacts may derive from the Milestone Plan, including:

- Release Plans; and
- Epics;

The Milestone Plan communicates measurable product outcomes rather than delivery scope.

---

# 10. AI Assistance

AI may assist in:

- analysing Product Roadmaps;
- identifying measurable product outcomes;
- recommending milestone sequencing;
- analysing milestone dependencies;
- assessing milestone impacts;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- preparing drafts for human review; and
- validating milestone consistency.

Milestone definition remains human-owned.

Approval of the Milestone Plan remains a human responsibility.

---

# 11. Conformance

A Milestone Plan conforms to this methodology when:

- it follows the defined development process;
- it has an identified human owner;
- it is traceable to the Product Vision;
- it is traceable to approved Product Decision Records;
- it is traceable to the Product Roadmap;
- it has undergone appropriate review;
- it has been formally approved; and
- it provides a governed product outcome planning foundation for subsequent product artifacts.
