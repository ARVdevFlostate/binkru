# Epic Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This methodology defines the recommended approach for creating and maintaining Product-defined draft Epics within the Product System.

An Epic represents a coherent body of business capability that contributes to the successful delivery of an approved Release Plan. It provides the initial decomposition of committed Product Capabilities into business-oriented deliverables before collaborative refinement with the Engineering System.

This methodology governs the creation of draft Epics. It does not govern engineering refinement, implementation planning or engineering execution.

The structure of the Epic artifact is defined by the Epic Template.

---

# 2. Scope

This methodology applies to all Epic artifacts governed by the Product System.

It is applicable whenever an approved Release Plan is decomposed into one or more draft Epics.

This methodology is intended for:

- Product Owners
- Product Managers
- Product Contributors
- Domain Experts
- AI assistants supporting product planning

Engineering participates only after draft Epics enter the Collaboration System.

---

# 3. Principles

An Epic shall:

- originate from an approved Release Plan;
- represent a coherent business capability;
- preserve approved business intent;
- remain implementation-independent;
- provide clear business boundaries;
- preserve traceability to upstream Product artifacts; and
- be ready to enter the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement within the Collaboration System.

---

# 4. Responsibilities

## Product

Product is responsible for:

- identifying Product Capabilities requiring Epic decomposition;
- defining draft Epics;
- preserving approved business intent;
- validating business scope and boundaries;
- maintaining traceability; and
- approving draft Epics.

---

## Contributors

Product Contributors and Domain Experts may assist in:

- identifying business capabilities;
- refining business scope;
- identifying dependencies;
- validating business outcomes.

---

## AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- recommending Epic decomposition;
- analysing Product Capabilities;
- identifying dependencies;
- validating traceability;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- preparing draft Epics; and
- supporting product analysis.

AI does not approve draft Epics.

---

# 5. Inputs

## Required Inputs

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Records
- Product Roadmap
- Milestone Plan
- Release Plan

---

## Optional Inputs

Where appropriate, Epic creation may consider:

- customer feedback;
- market insights;
- business constraints;
- regulatory considerations;
- previous Product artifacts; and
- lessons learned.

---

# 6. Method

Draft Epics should be developed through an iterative product planning process.

Typical activities include:

1. Review the approved Release Plan.
2. Review committed Product Capabilities.
3. Identify coherent business capabilities.
4. Define draft Epics.
5. Validate business scope.
6. Identify business dependencies.
7. Verify traceability.
8. Review draft Epics.
9. Approve draft Epics.

This methodology focuses on business decomposition rather than engineering decomposition.

---

# 7. Review and Approval

Draft Epics should be reviewed for:

- alignment with the approved Release Plan;
- preservation of approved business intent;
- business value;
- clarity of business scope;
- traceability;
- completeness; and
- governance compliance.

Approved draft Epics become the Product System input to the Collaboration System.

---

# 8. Evolution

Draft Epics may evolve during Product planning before entering the Collaboration System.

Once Epic Refinement begins, further evolution is governed collaboratively through the Collaboration System.

Historical versions shall remain part of the Product history.

---

# 9. Relationship to Other Artifacts

Draft Epics derive from the approved Release Plan.

Following Product approval, draft Epics become inputs to the Engineering Transition and Epic Refinement lifecycles within the Collaboration System.

Engineering-ready Epics subsequently become inputs to the Engineering System for Engineering planning and governed realization.

---

# 10. AI Assistance

AI may assist in:

- analysing Product Capabilities;
- recommending Epic decomposition;
- analysing business dependencies;
- validating traceability;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- preparing draft Epics; and
- supporting Product review.

Approval of draft Epics remains a human responsibility.

---

# 11. Conformance

An Epic conforms to this methodology when:

- it follows the defined methodology;
- it has an identified Product owner;
- it is traceable to the approved Release Plan;
- it preserves approved business intent;
- it has undergone Product review;
- it has been approved as a draft Epic; and
- it is ready to enter the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement.
