# Release Plan Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This methodology defines the recommended approach for developing, reviewing and maintaining a Release Plan within the Product System.

A Release Plan translates measurable product outcomes into governed Product intent for a specific release by defining its intended Product scope and objectives. It provides a structured approach for planning incremental Product evolution while maintaining alignment with the Product Vision and strategic Product planning.

This methodology describes the process for creating a Release Plan. It does not define the structure of the artifact, which is specified by the Release Plan Template.

---

# 2. Scope

This methodology applies to the creation, review and evolution of Release Plans governed by the Product System.

It is intended for:

- Product Owners
- Product Managers
- Product Leadership
- Product Contributors
- AI assistants supporting product development

---

# 3. Principles

A Release Plan should:

- translate measurable product outcomes into intended release scope;
- define a clear Product release intent and expectations;
- prioritise customer and business value;
- remain grounded in known constraints;
- provide a governed Product foundation for subsequent Product artifacts; and
- remain adaptable until formally approved.

---

# 4. Responsibilities

The Product Owner is responsible for:

- defining the Release Plan;
- ensuring alignment with the Product Vision, Product Roadmap and Milestone Plan;
- approving release scope;
- approving revisions; and
- maintaining the Release Plan throughout the product lifecycle.

Contributors may participate in release planning, scope definition and dependency analysis.

AI may assist with release analysis, dependency analysis, release balancing and impact assessment but does not determine release scope or approve the Release Plan.

---

# 5. Inputs

A Release Plan may be developed using information available at the time of creation.

## Required Inputs

The following inputs are required:

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Records
- Product Roadmap
- Milestone Plan

The Release Plan should reflect approved strategic direction, product outcomes and milestone planning.

## Optional Inputs

Where available, the following inputs may assist in developing a Release Plan:

- business objectives;
- customer research;
- stakeholder feedback;
- market analysis;
- organisational priorities;
- known constraints relevant to the planned release;
- lessons learned;
- previous Release Plans; and
- known dependencies relevant to the planned release.

The relevance and availability of these inputs depends on the product context.

---

# 6. Method

A Release Plan should be developed through an iterative process.

Typical activities include:

1. Review the Product Vision.
2. Review approved Product Decision Records.
3. Review the Product Roadmap.
4. Review the Milestone Plan.
5. Identify release scope.
6. Evaluate release dependencies.
7. Balance release scope against constraints.
8. Review Release Plan readiness.
9. Refine the Release Plan based on feedback.
10. Approve the Release Plan.

The methodology encourages value-based delivery rather than feature accumulation.

---

# 7. Review and Approval

A Release Plan should be reviewed for:

- alignment with the Product Vision;
- consistency with approved Product Decision Records;
- alignment with the Product Roadmap;
- alignment with the Milestone Plan;
- clarity of release scope;
- consistency with known constraints and dependencies;
- completeness;
- Product Release Plan readiness; and
- governance compliance.

The approved Release Plan becomes the authoritative Product planning artifact for the intended scope and objectives of the release.

---

# 8. Evolution

A Release Plan is expected to evolve until release scope has been approved.

Changes should reflect evolving Product understanding, milestone progress and known constraints relevant to the planned release.

Superseded Release Plan versions remain part of the product history.

---

# 9. Relationship to Other Artifacts

A Release Plan derives from the Product Vision, approved Product Decision Records, Product Roadmap and Milestone Plan.

Subsequent product artifacts may derive from the Release Plan, including:

- Epics;

The Release Plan communicates Product release intent and expectations rather than implementation activities or Engineering delivery commitments.

---

# 10. AI Assistance

AI may assist in:

- analysing milestone outcomes;
- identifying candidate release scope;
- analysing release dependencies;
- assessing release balance;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- preparing drafts for human review; and
- validating release consistency.

Product release intent, scope and expectations remain human-defined.

Approval of the Release Plan remains a human responsibility.

---

# 11. Conformance

A Release Plan conforms to this methodology when:

- it follows the defined development process;
- it has an identified human owner;
- it is traceable to the Product Vision;
- it is traceable to approved Product Decision Records;
- it is traceable to the Product Roadmap;
- it is traceable to the Milestone Plan;
- it has undergone appropriate review;
- it has been formally approved; and
- it provides a governed Product planning foundation for subsequent Product artifacts.
