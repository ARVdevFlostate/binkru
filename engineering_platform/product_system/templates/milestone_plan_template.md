# 1. Milestone Plan Summary

Provide a concise summary of the Milestone Plan.

The summary should communicate the intended progression of measurable product outcomes without describing delivery scope.

---

# 2. Strategic Context

Describe how the Milestone Plan supports:

- the Product Vision;
- approved Product Decision Records; and
- the Product Roadmap.

Explain the strategic context for the milestones defined in this plan.

---

# 3. Milestones

Document the milestones included in this plan.

For each milestone, describe:

- Milestone Name
- Description
- Strategic Initiative(s)
- Expected Product Outcome
- Success Criteria
- Dependencies (if applicable)
- Target Timeframe (optional)

A milestone represents a measurable product outcome rather than a delivery activity.

---

# 4. Milestone Sequencing

Describe the rationale used to determine the sequence of milestones.

Explain how milestone ordering supports strategic product evolution.

---

# 5. Assumptions

Document assumptions upon which the Milestone Plan depends.

---

# 6. Constraints

Document constraints that influence the Milestone Plan.

---

# 7. Risks

Document significant risks that may affect successful achievement of the milestones.

---

# 8. Success Characteristics

Describe the characteristics that indicate successful completion of the Milestone Plan.

Focus on measurable product outcomes rather than delivery activities.
