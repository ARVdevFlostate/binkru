# 1. Product Purpose

Describe the fundamental purpose of the product.

Explain why the product exists and the problem it intends to solve.

---

# 2. Product Direction

Describe the intended long-term direction of the product.

Focus on the desired future state rather than implementation details.

---

# 3. Customer Value

Describe the value the product intends to create for its intended customers or users.

---

# 4. Product Principles

Describe the fundamental principles that should guide product evolution.

These principles should remain relatively stable throughout the product lifecycle.

---

# 5. Success Characteristics

Describe the characteristics that would indicate the Product Vision has been realised.

These characteristics should describe outcomes rather than implementation milestones.

---

# 6. Assumptions

Document significant assumptions upon which the Product Vision depends.

Assumptions may evolve as product understanding improves.

---

# 7. Constraints

Document significant business, organisational or environmental constraints that influence the Product Vision.
