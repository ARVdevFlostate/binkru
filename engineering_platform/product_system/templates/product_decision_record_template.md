# 1. Decision Summary

Provide a concise summary of the product decision.

The summary should clearly describe the decision without explaining its implementation.

---

# 2. Decision Context

Describe the product context that led to this decision.

Include relevant background information necessary to understand why the decision is being considered.

---

# 3. Decision

Describe the product decision.

State the decision clearly and unambiguously.

---

# 4. Decision Rationale

Explain why this decision was selected.

Describe the reasoning that supports the decision.

---

# 5. Alternatives Considered

Summarise significant alternatives that were considered.

Where appropriate, briefly explain why they were not selected.

---

# 6. Expected Outcomes

Describe the intended outcomes of this decision.

Focus on product outcomes rather than implementation activities.

---

# 7. Impacts

Describe significant impacts resulting from this decision.

Impacts may include:

- customer impacts;
- business impacts;
- operational impacts;
- technical impacts; and
- regulatory impacts.

---

# 8. Assumptions

Document assumptions upon which the decision depends.

---

# 9. Constraints

Document constraints that influenced the decision.
