# 1. Roadmap Summary

Provide a concise summary of the Product Roadmap.

The summary should communicate the strategic direction of the roadmap without describing implementation activities.

---

# 2. Strategic Context

Describe the strategic context for this roadmap.

Explain how the roadmap supports the Product Vision and approved Product Decision Records.

---

# 3. Strategic Initiatives

Document the strategic initiatives included in this roadmap.

For each initiative, describe:

- Initiative Name
- Description
- Strategic Objective
- Priority
- Dependencies (if applicable)
- Target Timeframe (optional)

The roadmap communicates strategic intent rather than delivery commitments.

---

# 4. Initiative Prioritisation

Describe the rationale used to prioritise the initiatives.

Explain why the initiatives appear in their current order.

---

# 5. Assumptions

Document assumptions upon which the roadmap depends.

---

# 6. Constraints

Document constraints that influence the roadmap.

---

# 7. Risks

Document significant risks that may affect successful execution of the roadmap.

---

# 8. Success Characteristics

Describe the outcomes that indicate successful execution of the roadmap.

Focus on strategic outcomes rather than delivery milestones.
