# Product Lifecycle Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the Product Lifecycle governed by the Product System.

The Product Lifecycle describes the progressive evolution of Product knowledge from strategic intent to governed Product artifacts that are ready to enter the Collaboration System.

It establishes the authoritative lifecycle relationships between Product artifacts while preserving business intent, governance and traceability.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to the Product Lifecycle governed by the Product System.

It defines:

- lifecycle stages;
- lifecycle progression;
- governance responsibilities;
- lifecycle relationships;
- lifecycle boundaries; and
- lifecycle conformance.

This specification governs Product lifecycle progression only.

Collaboration and Engineering lifecycle progression are outside the scope of this specification.

---

# 3. Lifecycle Principles

The Product Lifecycle is governed by the following principles.

- Product lifecycle progression shall preserve approved business intent.
- Product lifecycle progression shall be governed.
- Product artifacts shall evolve through progressively greater business definition.
- Traceability shall be preserved throughout lifecycle progression.
- Product governance concludes with approved draft Epics.
- Subsequent lifecycle progression is governed by the Collaboration System.

---

# 4. Product Lifecycle

The Product System defines the following lifecycle progression.

```text
Vision
    │
    ▼
Product Decision Records
    │
    ▼
Roadmap
    │
    ▼
Milestone Plans
    │
    ▼
Release Plans
    │
    ▼
Draft Epics
```

Each stage elaborates approved Product knowledge without contradicting governing Product artifacts.

---

# 5. Lifecycle Stages

## Vision

Defines long-term Product purpose and direction.

---

## Product Decision Records

Capture strategic Product decisions supporting the Vision.

---

## Roadmap

Translates Product Vision and Product Decisions into planned Product evolution.

---

## Milestone Plans

Organise Product evolution into significant intermediate objectives.

---

## Release Plans

Define the scope and objectives of individual Product releases.

---

## Draft Epics

Represent coherent business capabilities derived from approved Release Plans.

Approved draft Epics conclude Product governance and become inputs to the Collaboration System.

---

# 6. Lifecycle Progression

Progression between lifecycle stages shall:

- preserve approved business intent;
- maintain governance;
- preserve traceability;
- increase Product definition;
- maintain Product ownership; and
- satisfy governing Product principles.

Lifecycle progression shall not bypass required stages unless explicitly governed.

---

# 7. Lifecycle Governance

Lifecycle progression requires:

- completion of governing Product methodologies;
- successful validation using Product checklists;
- Product approval;
- preserved traceability; and
- governance compliance.

Each stage shall satisfy its governing specification before progressing.

---

# 8. Lifecycle Boundaries

The Product Lifecycle concludes when draft Epics have been approved.

Following Product approval:

- draft Epics enter the Collaboration System;
- collaborative refinement is governed independently;
- Engineering execution remains outside Product governance.

The Product System does not govern engineering readiness or implementation.

---

# 9. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans remain responsible for:

- Product intent;
- Product decisions;
- Product approval;
- Product accountability.

AI may assist by:

- drafting Product artifacts;
- validating Product governance;
- analysing lifecycle consistency;
- recommending improvements;
- supporting lifecycle progression.

AI does not approve lifecycle progression.

---

# 10. Relationship to Other Systems

The Product Lifecycle precedes the Collaboration System.

```text
Product System
        │
        ▼
Governed Product Artifacts
        │
        ▼
Collaboration System
```

The Collaboration System governs the collaborative maturation of approved draft Epics.

Engineering execution is governed by the Engineering System.

---

# 11. Conformance

A Product Workflow conforms to the Product Lifecycle when:

- lifecycle stages are preserved;
- lifecycle progression follows governed Product practices;
- Product ownership is maintained;
- traceability is preserved;
- Product approvals have been obtained;
- Product governance has been completed; and
- approved draft Epics are ready to enter the Collaboration System.
