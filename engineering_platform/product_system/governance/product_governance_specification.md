# Product Governance Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the Product Governance model of the Product System.

Product Governance establishes the responsibilities, controls, approvals and governance practices that ensure Product artifacts preserve approved business intent throughout the Product Lifecycle.

This specification defines how Product governance is exercised independently of Collaboration and Engineering governance.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to every Product artifact governed by the Product System.

It defines:

- governance responsibilities;
- governance activities;
- governance controls;
- governance boundaries;
- governance authority; and
- governance conformance.

Collaboration governance and Engineering governance are outside the scope of this specification.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Product Governance shall:

- preserve approved business intent;
- ensure Product ownership;
- maintain Product accountability;
- preserve Product traceability;
- require governed lifecycle progression;
- establish Product approval before lifecycle progression;
- support AI-assisted Product development; and
- conclude with governed Product artifacts entering the Collaboration System.

---

# 4. Governance Responsibilities

## Product Ownership

Every Product artifact shall have an identified Product owner responsible for:

- correctness;
- business intent;
- governance compliance;
- lifecycle progression;
- Product approval; and
- ongoing maintenance.

---

## Product Review

Product artifacts shall undergo appropriate review before approval.

Review activities should confirm:

- alignment with governing Product artifacts;
- business consistency;
- completeness;
- traceability;
- governance compliance.

---

## Product Approval

Product approval confirms that:

- Product governance has been completed;
- business intent has been preserved;
- required Product reviews have been completed;
- required Product approvals have been obtained; and
- the artifact is ready to progress within the Product Lifecycle.

---

# 5. Governance Controls

Product Governance shall ensure:

- methodologies are followed;
- templates are used;
- checklists are completed;
- specifications are satisfied;
- traceability is preserved; and
- governance evidence is maintained.

Governance controls shall be applied consistently throughout the Product Lifecycle.

---

# 6. Governance Boundaries

The Product System governs Product artifacts only.

Product Governance concludes when approved draft Epics enter the Collaboration System.

Subsequent collaborative refinement is governed independently by the Collaboration System.

Engineering execution remains outside Product Governance.

---

# 7. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans remain accountable for:

- Product intent;
- Product ownership;
- Product decisions;
- Product approval;
- Product governance.

AI may assist by:

- drafting Product artifacts;
- validating Product governance;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- analysing traceability;
- recommending improvements;
- supporting Product reviews.

AI does not approve Product artifacts.

---

# 8. Relationship to Other Systems

The Product System governs business intent.

The Collaboration System governs collaborative refinement.

The Engineering System governs engineering execution.

Governance responsibilities transition between systems while preserving artifact identity and traceability.

---

# 9. Governance Evidence

Evidence of Product Governance should include:

- approved Product artifacts;
- completed Product reviews;
- Product approvals;
- completed Product checklists;
- preserved traceability;
- governance history.

Governance evidence shall remain available throughout the Product Lifecycle.

---

# 10. Conformance

A Product Workflow conforms to this specification when:

- Product ownership has been established;
- Product governance responsibilities have been fulfilled;
- Product reviews have been completed;
- Product approvals have been obtained;
- governance controls have been satisfied;
- traceability has been preserved;
- governance evidence has been maintained; and
- governed Product artifacts are ready to enter the Collaboration System.
