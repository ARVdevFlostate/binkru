# Product Principles Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the governing principles of the Product System.

These principles establish the fundamental characteristics expected of all Product artifacts, Product methodologies and Product governance activities.

All Product System assets shall conform to these principles.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to every artifact, methodology, template, checklist and composition specification governed by the Product System.

The principles defined in this specification are independent of individual products, technologies and implementation approaches.

---

# 3. Governing Principles

## Product Intent

Every Product artifact shall preserve approved business intent.

Lower-level Product artifacts shall elaborate business intent without contradicting governing Product artifacts.

---

## Product Ownership

Every Product artifact shall have a single authoritative Product owner.

Ownership includes responsibility for correctness, governance, lifecycle progression and approval.

---

## Product Governance

Every Product artifact shall be created, reviewed and approved through governed Product practices.

Product governance concludes with approved draft Epics entering the Collaboration System.

---

## Traceability

Every Product artifact shall preserve traceability to one or more governing Product artifacts.

Traceability shall remain intact throughout Product governance.

---

## Progressive Elaboration

Product artifacts shall evolve from strategic intent toward increasingly detailed business definition.

Each artifact shall provide additional clarity without duplicating responsibilities.

---

## Separation of Responsibilities

The Product System governs business intent.

The Collaboration System governs collaborative refinement.

The Engineering System governs engineering execution.

Product artifacts shall not define engineering implementation.

---

## Product Quality

Every Product artifact shall satisfy the quality requirements defined by its governing methodology, template, checklist and specification.

Quality shall be established before Product approval.

---

## Human Accountability

Humans remain accountable for:

- Product intent;
- Product decisions;
- Product approval; and
- Product governance.

AI assistance does not replace Product accountability.

---

## AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- drafting Product artifacts;
- analysing alternatives;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- validating traceability;
- recommending improvements; and
- supporting governed Product practices.

AI does not approve Product artifacts.

---

## Technology Independence

The Product System shall remain independent of implementation technologies, programming languages and engineering approaches.

Product governance defines business intent rather than technical implementation.

---

# 4. Principle Relationships

The governing principles operate collectively.

```text
Business Intent
        │
        ▼
Ownership
        │
        ▼
Governance
        │
        ▼
Traceability
        │
        ▼
Quality
        │
        ▼
Governed Product Artifact
```

Each principle reinforces the integrity of Product governance.

---

# 5. Relationship to Other Systems

The Product Principles govern only the Product System.

Following Product approval, draft Epics enter the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement.

Engineering execution is governed independently by the Engineering System.

---

# 6. Conformance

A Product artifact conforms to this specification when:

- approved business intent has been preserved;
- Product ownership has been established;
- Product governance has been followed;
- traceability has been maintained;
- quality requirements have been satisfied;
- required Product approvals have been obtained; and
- the artifact is consistent with all governing Product principles.
