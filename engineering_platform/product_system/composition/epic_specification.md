# Epic Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the required structure and characteristics of an Epic within the Product System.

An Epic is a Product-owned artifact that represents a coherent body of business capability derived from an approved Release Plan.

The Epic serves as the final Product artifact before work enters the Collaboration System for collaborative refinement with Engineering.

This specification defines what an Epic shall contain. It does not define how an Epic is created, reviewed or refined.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to all Epic artifacts governed by the Product System.

It defines the minimum information required for an Epic to support Product governance, business traceability and subsequent collaborative refinement.

---

# 3. Objectives

An Epic shall:

- represent a coherent business capability;
- preserve approved business intent;
- provide clear business scope;
- define expected business outcomes;
- preserve traceability to upstream Product artifacts;
- support Product governance; and
- provide sufficient business context for collaborative refinement.

---

# 4. Required Information

Every Epic shall include:

## Artifact Information

- Artifact Identifier
- Epic Identifier
- Product
- Version
- Status
- Product Owner
- Creation Date
- Last Updated

---

## Business Summary

- Epic Name
- Business Objective
- Summary

---

## Business Context

- Product Capability
- Release Plan reference
- Related Product artifacts

---

## Business Scope

- Business capability
- Included scope
- Excluded scope
- Business constraints
- Business assumptions

---

## Expected Outcomes

- Customer outcomes
- Business outcomes
- Operational outcomes
- Success criteria

---

## Business Dependencies

Business dependencies relevant to successful delivery.

---

## Business Risks

Business risks requiring visibility before collaborative refinement.

---

## Open Questions

Outstanding questions requiring clarification before the Epic enters the Collaboration System.

---

## Related Epics

Relationships with other Product-defined Epics.

---

## Product Review

Product review observations.

---

## Product Approval

Evidence that the draft Epic has been approved by Product.

---

## Revision History

Version history for the Epic.

---

# 5. Traceability

Every Epic shall preserve traceability to:

- Product Vision;
- Product Decision Records;
- Product Roadmap;
- Milestone Plan; and
- Release Plan.

Traceability shall remain intact throughout the Epic lifecycle.

---

# 6. Quality Characteristics

A conforming Epic shall be:

## Business Focused

The Epic shall describe business capability rather than implementation.

---

## Complete

Required business information shall be present.

---

## Consistent

Business intent shall remain consistent with upstream Product artifacts.

---

## Traceable

Relationships to upstream Product artifacts shall be explicit.

---

## Reviewable

Product stakeholders shall be able to review the Epic without requiring engineering knowledge.

---

## Collaboration Ready

The Epic shall provide sufficient business information to support collaborative refinement within the Collaboration System.

---

# 7. Status

Within the Product System an Epic may exist in one of the following states:

- Draft
- Approved

Further lifecycle progression is governed by the Collaboration System and Engineering System.

---

# 8. Relationship to Other Artifacts

The Epic derives from:

- Release Plan

The Epic references:

- Product Capability

Following Product approval the Epic becomes an input to the Collaboration System.

The Collaboration System governs the collaborative maturation of the draft Epic into an Engineering-ready Epic.

The Engineering System subsequently consumes Engineering-ready Epics for Engineering planning and governed realization.

---

# 9. AI Assistance

AI may assist in:

- analysing Product Capabilities;
- recommending Epic decomposition;
- validating business consistency;
- identifying dependencies;
- validating traceability; and
- preparing draft content.

AI assistance shall not replace Product accountability or Product approval.

---

# 10. Conformance

An Epic conforms to this specification when:

- all required information is present;
- business intent is preserved;
- business scope is clearly defined;
- expected outcomes have been documented;
- traceability has been established;
- Product review has been completed;
- Product approval has been recorded; and
- the Epic is ready to enter the Collaboration System.
