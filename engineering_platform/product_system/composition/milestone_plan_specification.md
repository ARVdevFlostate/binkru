# Milestone Plan Composition Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how a Milestone Plan is composed within the Product System.

It establishes the governed inputs, composition process and expected output for producing a Milestone Plan artifact.

This specification supports consistent composition by both humans and AI assistants.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to all Milestone Plan artifacts governed by the Product System.

It complements:

- Milestone Plan Methodology
- Milestone Plan Template
- Milestone Plan Checklist
- Product Artifact Model

---

# 3. Composition Principles

Composition of a Milestone Plan shall:

- produce a complete Milestone Plan artifact;
- conform to the Milestone Plan Template;
- follow the Milestone Plan Methodology;
- satisfy the Milestone Plan Checklist;
- align with the Product Vision;
- align with approved Product Decision Records;
- align with the Product Roadmap;
- preserve milestone traceability; and
- preserve human ownership of milestone planning.

---

# 4. Composition Inputs

A Milestone Plan may be composed using:

## Required Inputs

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Records
- Product Roadmap
- Milestone Plan Template
- Milestone Plan Methodology

The Milestone Plan shall reflect the approved strategic direction, product decisions and roadmap initiatives.

## Optional Inputs

Where available, composition may consider:

- previous Milestone Plans;
- business objectives;
- customer research;
- stakeholder feedback;
- market analysis;
- organisational priorities;
- product constraints;
- lessons learned; and
- strategic dependencies.

The availability of optional inputs depends on the product context.

---

# 5. Composition Process

Composition should generally perform the following activities:

1. Review the Product Vision.
2. Review approved Product Decision Records.
3. Review the Product Roadmap.
4. Identify measurable product outcomes.
5. Define significant milestones.
6. Associate milestones with strategic initiatives.
7. Define success criteria.
8. Determine milestone sequencing.
9. Identify milestone dependencies.
10. Document assumptions, constraints and risks.
11. Identify related artifacts.
12. Populate the Milestone Plan Template.
13. Validate against the Milestone Plan Checklist.

Composition is iterative and may revisit earlier activities as product understanding evolves.

---

# 6. Composition Output

The output of composition is a completed Milestone Plan that:

- conforms to the Milestone Plan Template;
- satisfies the Milestone Plan Checklist;
- aligns with the Product Vision;
- aligns with approved Product Decision Records;
- aligns with the Product Roadmap;
- preserves milestone traceability;
- establishes an authoritative product outcome planning artifact; and
- is ready for Product Owner review and approval.

---

# 7. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans are responsible for:

- defining measurable product outcomes;
- defining milestones;
- determining milestone priorities and sequencing;
- validating strategic alignment; and
- approving the Milestone Plan.

AI may assist by:

- analysing Product Roadmaps;
- identifying measurable product outcomes;
- recommending milestone groupings;
- recommending milestone sequencing;
- analysing milestone dependencies;
- assessing milestone impacts;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- checking completeness;
- validating traceability; and
- validating conformance.

AI does not determine milestone priorities or approve the Milestone Plan.

---

# 8. Relationships

This specification composes a Milestone Plan using governed Product System assets.

Each Milestone Plan shall be traceable to:

- the Product Vision;
- approved Product Decision Records; and
- the Product Roadmap.

Completed Milestone Plans become governed inputs to subsequent product artifacts including:

- Release Plans; and
- Epics;

The Milestone Plan communicates measurable product outcomes rather than delivery scope.

---

# 9. Conformance

Composition conforms to this specification when:

- required inputs have been considered;
- the Milestone Plan Template has been completed;
- the Milestone Plan Methodology has been followed;
- the Milestone Plan Checklist has been satisfied;
- milestone traceability has been preserved;
- human ownership has been preserved; and
- the Milestone Plan is ready for approval.
