# Product Roadmap Composition Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how a Product Roadmap is composed within the Product System.

It establishes the governed inputs, composition process and expected output for producing a Product Roadmap artifact.

This specification supports consistent composition by both humans and AI assistants.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to all Product Roadmap artifacts governed by the Product System.

It complements:

- Roadmap Methodology
- Roadmap Template
- Roadmap Checklist
- Product Artifact Model

---

# 3. Composition Principles

Composition of a Product Roadmap shall:

- produce a complete Product Roadmap artifact;
- conform to the Roadmap Template;
- follow the Roadmap Methodology;
- satisfy the Roadmap Checklist;
- align with the Product Vision;
- align with approved Product Decision Records;
- preserve strategic initiative traceability; and
- preserve human ownership of product priorities.

---

# 4. Composition Inputs

A Product Roadmap may be composed using:

## Required Inputs

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Records
- Roadmap Template
- Roadmap Methodology

The Product Roadmap shall reflect the approved strategic direction and product decisions.

## Optional Inputs

Where available, composition may consider:

- previous Product Roadmaps;
- business objectives;
- customer research;
- stakeholder feedback;
- market analysis;
- organisational priorities;
- product constraints;
- lessons learned; and
- strategic dependencies.

The availability of optional inputs depends on the product context.

---

# 5. Composition Process

Composition should generally perform the following activities:

1. Review the Product Vision.
2. Review approved Product Decision Records.
3. Identify strategic initiatives.
4. Group related Product Decisions into initiatives.
5. Determine initiative priorities.
6. Determine initiative sequencing.
7. Identify dependencies.
8. Document assumptions, constraints and risks.
9. Define success characteristics.
10. Identify related artifacts.
11. Populate the Roadmap Template.
12. Validate against the Roadmap Checklist.

Composition is iterative and may revisit earlier activities as product understanding evolves.

---

# 6. Composition Output

The output of composition is a completed Product Roadmap that:

- conforms to the Roadmap Template;
- satisfies the Roadmap Checklist;
- aligns with the Product Vision;
- aligns with approved Product Decision Records;
- preserves strategic initiative traceability;
- establishes an authoritative strategic planning artifact; and
- is ready for Product Owner review and approval.

---

# 7. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans are responsible for:

- defining strategic initiatives;
- determining product priorities;
- validating strategic direction; and
- approving the Product Roadmap.

AI may assist by:

- analysing Product Decision Records;
- identifying initiative groupings;
- recommending prioritisation;
- analysing dependencies;
- assessing roadmap impacts;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- checking completeness;
- validating traceability; and
- validating conformance.

AI does not determine product priorities or approve the Product Roadmap.

---

# 8. Relationships

This specification composes a Product Roadmap using governed Product System assets.

Each Product Roadmap shall be traceable to:

- the Product Vision; and
- approved Product Decision Records.

Completed Product Roadmaps become governed inputs to subsequent product artifacts including:

- Milestone Plans;
- Release Plans; and
- Epics;

The Product Roadmap communicates strategic direction rather than delivery commitments.

---

# 9. Conformance

Composition conforms to this specification when:

- required inputs have been considered;
- the Roadmap Template has been completed;
- the Roadmap Methodology has been followed;
- the Roadmap Checklist has been satisfied;
- strategic initiative traceability has been preserved;
- human ownership has been preserved; and
- the Product Roadmap is ready for approval.
