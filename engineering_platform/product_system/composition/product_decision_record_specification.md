# Product Decision Record Composition Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how a Product Decision Record is composed within the Product System.

It establishes the governed inputs, composition process and expected output for producing a Product Decision Record artifact.

This specification supports consistent composition by both humans and AI assistants.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to all Product Decision Record artifacts governed by the Product System.

It complements:

- Product Decision Record Methodology
- Product Decision Record Template
- Product Decision Record Checklist
- Product Artifact Model

---

# 3. Composition Principles

Composition of a Product Decision Record shall:

- produce a complete Product Decision Record artifact;
- conform to the Product Decision Record Template;
- follow the Product Decision Record Methodology;
- satisfy the Product Decision Record Checklist;
- align with the Product Vision;
- preserve decision traceability; and
- preserve human ownership of product decisions.

---

# 4. Composition Inputs

A Product Decision Record may be composed using:

## Required Inputs

- Product Vision
- Product Decision Record Template
- Product Decision Record Methodology

## Optional Inputs

Where available, composition may consider:

- existing Product Decision Records;
- business objectives;
- customer research;
- stakeholder feedback;
- market analysis;
- technical constraints;
- regulatory considerations; and
- lessons learned.

The availability of optional inputs depends on the product context.

---

# 5. Composition Process

Composition should generally perform the following activities:

1. Identify the product decision.
2. Establish the decision context.
3. Evaluate available information.
4. Identify alternatives where appropriate.
5. Determine the preferred decision.
6. Document the decision rationale.
7. Assess expected outcomes.
8. Assess decision impacts.
9. Document assumptions and constraints.
10. Identify related artifacts.
11. Populate the Product Decision Record Template.
12. Validate against the Product Decision Record Checklist.

Composition is iterative and may revisit earlier activities as product understanding evolves.

---

# 6. Composition Output

The output of composition is a completed Product Decision Record that:

- conforms to the Product Decision Record Template;
- satisfies the Product Decision Record Checklist;
- aligns with the Product Vision;
- preserves decision traceability;
- establishes an authoritative record of the product decision; and
- is ready for Product Owner review and approval.

---

# 7. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans are responsible for:

- defining significant product decisions;
- evaluating business context;
- approving product decisions.

AI may assist by:

- analysing available information;
- identifying decision alternatives;
- assessing impacts;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- improving clarity;
- checking completeness;
- validating traceability; and
- validating conformance.

AI does not determine product strategy or approve product decisions.

---

# 8. Relationships

This specification composes a Product Decision Record using governed Product System assets.

Each Product Decision Record shall be traceable to the Product Vision.

Completed Product Decision Records become governed inputs to subsequent product artifacts including:

- Roadmaps;
- Milestone Plans;
- Release Plans; and
- Epics;

Multiple Product Decision Records may contribute to the same downstream artifact.

---

# 9. Conformance

Composition conforms to this specification when:

- required inputs have been considered;
- the Product Decision Record Template has been completed;
- the Product Decision Record Methodology has been followed;
- the Product Decision Record Checklist has been satisfied;
- decision traceability has been preserved;
- human ownership has been preserved; and
- the Product Decision Record is ready for approval.
