# Product Vision Composition Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how a Product Vision is composed within the Product System.

It establishes the governed inputs, composition process and expected output for producing a Product Vision artifact.

This specification supports consistent composition by both humans and AI assistants.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to all Product Vision artifacts governed by the Product System.

It complements:

- Vision Methodology
- Vision Template
- Vision Checklist
- Product Artifact Model

---

# 3. Composition Principles

Composition of a Product Vision shall:

- produce a complete Product Vision artifact;
- conform to the Vision Template;
- follow the Vision Methodology;
- satisfy the Vision Checklist;
- align with Product Principles; and
- preserve human ownership of product intent.

---

# 4. Composition Inputs

A Product Vision may be composed using:

## Required Inputs

- Vision Template
- Vision Methodology

## Optional Inputs

Where available, composition may consider:

- Product Principles
- Product Decision Records
- Business objectives
- Customer research
- Market analysis
- Organisational strategy
- Existing product documentation
- Stakeholder feedback

The availability of optional inputs depends on the product context.

---

# 5. Composition Process

Composition should generally perform the following activities:

1. Establish product context.
2. Identify the product purpose.
3. Determine intended customer value.
4. Define long-term product direction.
5. Identify product principles.
6. Document assumptions.
7. Document constraints.
8. Identify related artifacts.
9. Populate the Vision Template.
10. Validate against the Vision Checklist.

Composition is iterative and may revisit earlier activities as product understanding evolves.

---

# 6. Composition Output

The output of composition is a completed Product Vision that:

- conforms to the Vision Template;
- satisfies the Vision Checklist;
- aligns with Product Principles;
- establishes authoritative product intent; and
- is ready for Product Owner review and approval.

---

# 7. Human and AI Responsibilities

Humans are responsible for:

- defining strategic product intent;
- validating business direction;
- approving the Product Vision.

AI may assist by:

- analysing available inputs;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- drafting content;
- improving clarity;
- checking completeness;
- validating conformance.

AI does not determine product strategy or approve the Product Vision.

---

# 8. Relationships

This specification composes the Product Vision using governed Product System assets.

The completed Product Vision becomes the governing input for subsequent product artifacts including:

- Product Decision Records;
- Roadmaps;
- Milestone Plans;
- Release Plans; and
- Epics;

---

# 9. Conformance

Composition conforms to this specification when:

- required inputs have been considered;
- the Vision Template has been completed;
- the Vision Methodology has been followed;
- the Vision Checklist has been satisfied;
- human ownership has been preserved; and
- the Product Vision is ready for approval.
