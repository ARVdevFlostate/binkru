# Release Admission Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the governed Engineering–Release collaboration through which one or more concluded Engineering outcomes are determined to be eligible to enter Release governance for a defined Release purpose.

It establishes the semantic boundary, participation, authoritative inputs, determination, scope, authority, traceability, provenance, and conformance obligations applicable to Release Admission.

Release Admission provides a governed cross-system boundary between concluded Engineering realization and Release governance without transferring or redefining the authoritative semantics of either system.

---

## 2. Scope

This specification governs:

- the meaning of Release Admission;
- ownership of the Release Admission collaboration interaction;
- participating systems and authorities;
- Release Admission entry conditions;
- authoritative Engineering and Release-side inputs;
- the Release Admission basis;
- the Release Admission determination;
- admitted Release scope;
- relationships to Capability Acceptance, Release Candidate formation, Release Readiness, and Release Authorization;
- additional Engineering outcomes;
- governed return to Engineering and subsequent re-entry;
- continued applicability and reassessment;
- authority resolution;
- traceability and provenance;
- preservation of Release Admission;
- human, AI, and automation participation; and
- Release Admission conformance.

This specification does not govern:

- Product intent or Product Capability;
- Capability Acceptance;
- Engineering planning or realization;
- Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion;
- Release Candidate formation;
- Candidate Integrity;
- Release Fingerprint establishment;
- Release Validation;
- Release Readiness;
- Release Authorization;
- Release Promotion;
- Release Recovery;
- Released State; or
- Release Conclusion.

Those semantics remain governed by their applicable authoritative systems and specifications.

---

## 3. Release Admission

Release Admission is the governed determination that one or more concluded Engineering outcomes are eligible to enter Release governance for a defined Release purpose.

Release Admission establishes eligibility to enter Release governance.

Release Admission does not establish that the admitted Engineering outcomes:

- satisfy Product intent;
- form a Release Candidate;
- satisfy Candidate Integrity;
- satisfy Release Readiness;
- are authorized for Release Promotion;
- have been successfully promoted;
- have established Released State; or
- have reached Release Conclusion.

Release Admission SHALL remain distinguishable from all such downstream or independently governed determinations.

---

## 4. Collaboration Ownership

Release Admission is owned as a governed collaboration interaction by the Collaboration System's Engineering–Release collaboration domain.

The Collaboration System governs the Release Admission interaction and preservation of its resulting determination.

Ownership of the collaboration interaction SHALL NOT transfer ownership of participating systems' authoritative semantics to the Collaboration System.

The Collaboration System SHALL NOT acquire, redefine, broaden, narrow, or exercise Engineering or Release authority merely by governing the Release Admission interaction.

Participating systems retain ownership of their authoritative semantics.

---

## 5. System Boundaries

The Engineering System establishes and preserves applicable Engineering truth, including Engineering realization, Engineering Evidence, Engineering Conclusion, Engineering Completion, and the Engineering Delivery Record.

The Release System establishes and preserves applicable Release truth, including Release Identity, Release Candidates, Release Fingerprints, Candidate Integrity, Release Evidence, Release Readiness, Release Authorization, Release Promotion, Release Outcome, Released State, Release Recovery, Release Conclusion, and the Release Record.

The Collaboration System governs the cross-system interaction through which applicable concluded Engineering outcomes are considered for Release Admission.

Release Admission SHALL NOT redefine authoritative Product, Engineering, or Release semantics.

---

## 6. Participating Systems and Authorities

Release Admission requires participation sufficient to establish the applicable cross-system determination under governing authority.

Engineering participation provides the authoritative Engineering basis relevant to the proposed Admission.

Release participation provides the applicable Release purpose, Release identity, Release context, constraints, and other Release-side basis required for the proposed Admission.

Other authoritative participants MAY participate where required by applicable governance.

This specification does not prescribe organizational roles, job titles, team structures, or universal approval hierarchies.

Responsibility to participate SHALL NOT itself establish authority.

Technical capability to participate SHALL NOT itself establish authority.

---

## 7. Release Admission Entry Conditions

An Engineering outcome considered for Release Admission SHALL have reached an applicable Engineering Conclusion.

The authoritative Engineering basis required to establish that conclusion SHALL be determinable through applicable Engineering records, evidence, references, and provenance.

A Finalized Engineering Delivery Record MAY provide the Engineering basis for an applicable Release Admission determination.

Release Admission SHALL NOT be used to manufacture Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion for unfinished or unconcluded Engineering realization.

Where the required Engineering basis is absent, unresolved, or insufficient for the applicable Admission determination, Release Admission SHALL NOT infer or manufacture that basis.

---

## 8. Release Establishment Relationship

Release Admission occurs in relation to an established Release with a determinable Release Identity and Release purpose or governing basis.

Release establishment SHALL precede Release Admission.

Release Admission does not itself establish the Release.

Release Admission SHALL NOT manufacture or substitute for Release Identity.

The applicable Release Record MAY already have been initialized in accordance with Release System governance before Release Admission is established.

---

## 9. Release Admission Basis

The Release Admission basis comprises the authoritative inputs, applicable context, constraints, provenance, and participating authority required to determine whether identified concluded Engineering outcomes are eligible to enter Release governance for the defined Release purpose.

The Release Admission basis MAY draw upon authoritative inputs from multiple systems or governance domains.

Composition, resolution, presentation, or evaluation of the Release Admission basis SHALL NOT transfer semantic ownership of its constituent inputs.

The applicable source and authority of material inputs SHALL remain determinable.

---

## 10. Authoritative Engineering Inputs

Applicable Engineering inputs MAY include:

- identified concluded Engineering outcomes;
- Engineering Conclusion;
- Engineering Completion semantics where applicable;
- applicable Engineering Delivery Record or Records;
- applicable Engineering Evidence;
- known Engineering constraints;
- applicable realization conditions;
- relevant Engineering decisions;
- applicable provenance; and
- other Engineering-owned semantics relevant to Release Admission.

Engineering retains ownership of these semantics.

Release Admission SHALL consume Engineering inputs according to their established meaning and SHALL NOT reinterpret them as different Engineering conclusions, states, or decisions.

---

## 11. Release-Side Inputs

Applicable Release-side inputs MAY include:

- Release Identity;
- defined Release purpose;
- applicable Release context;
- applicable Release Exposure Context where already established or relevant;
- applicable Release constraints;
- intended Release progression basis where already known;
- applicable Release governance requirements; and
- other Release-owned semantics relevant to Release Admission.

Release-side inputs SHALL NOT be used through Release Admission to redefine the authoritative Engineering basis.

Release Admission SHALL NOT itself establish Release Candidate composition, Release Fingerprint, Release Readiness, or Release Authorization.

---

## 12. Other Authoritative Inputs

Release Admission MAY consume other authoritative inputs where required by applicable governance.

Such inputs MAY originate from Product or other applicable governance domains.

Consumption of an authoritative input SHALL NOT transfer ownership or authority over that input to the Collaboration System, Engineering System, or Release System.

The source, applicability, and authority of material cross-system inputs SHALL remain determinable.

---

## 13. Release Admission Determination

The Release Admission interaction establishes whether identified concluded Engineering outcomes are eligible to enter Release governance for the defined Release purpose.

A Release Admission determination MAY establish that the identified Engineering outcomes are:

- admitted for the defined Release purpose; or
- not admitted for the defined Release purpose.

These semantic outcomes do not require a universal representation or canonical enumeration.

Where no governed determination has yet been established, absence of a determination SHALL NOT be interpreted as either admission or non-admission.

A Release Admission determination SHALL preserve a determinable basis sufficient to explain the resulting determination.

---

## 14. Admission Scope

A Release Admission determination SHALL be scoped to the material basis for which it was established.

Applicable scope SHALL include or make determinable:

- Release Identity;
- defined Release purpose;
- identified concluded Engineering outcome or outcomes;
- authoritative Engineering basis;
- applicable Release-side basis;
- applicable constraints;
- participating authority or authorities;
- resulting determination;
- material rationale or basis; and
- provenance.

Release Admission SHALL NOT silently apply beyond the material scope for which its determination was established.

---

## 15. Admitted Release Scope

Where Release Admission establishes eligibility, the identified concluded Engineering outcomes become part of the admitted Release scope for the applicable Release and defined Release purpose.

Admitted Release Scope is Release lifecycle semantics.

It is not a separate canonical artifact family.

The Release System governs admitted Release scope after applicable Release Admission.

Admission into Release scope SHALL NOT itself establish Release Candidate composition.

---

## 16. Multiple Engineering Outcomes

A single Release Admission determination MAY consider one or more concluded Engineering outcomes where permitted by applicable governance.

Where multiple Engineering outcomes are considered together, the determination SHALL preserve sufficient scope and provenance to identify the Engineering outcomes to which the determination applies.

Admission of one Engineering outcome SHALL NOT imply admission of another Engineering outcome unless that outcome is within the governed scope of the applicable determination.

---

## 17. Additional Engineering Outcomes

Additional concluded Engineering outcomes MAY become relevant to an existing Release after prior Release Admission has occurred.

Such outcomes SHALL NOT silently become part of admitted Release scope or an existing Release Candidate.

Each additional concluded Engineering outcome SHALL enter Release governance only through applicable Release Admission.

Following such Admission, the Release System MAY perform applicable candidate reassessment, candidate replacement, evidence reassessment, readiness reassessment, authorization reassessment, or other Release-governed activity.

---

## 18. Capability Acceptance Relationship

Capability Acceptance remains a Product–Engineering Collaboration concern.

Capability Acceptance determines whether delivered capability satisfies applicable Product-owned intended capability or business intent.

Release Admission and Capability Acceptance are distinct governed determinations.

Capability Acceptance MAY provide an authoritative input or applicable condition for Release Admission where required by applicable governance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT be universally required for Release Admission unless applicable governance establishes that requirement.

Release Admission SHALL NOT establish, infer, redefine, or manufacture Capability Acceptance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself establish Release Admission.

---

## 19. Release Candidate Relationship

An Engineering outcome SHALL become part of governed Release Candidate composition only after applicable Release Admission has established its eligibility to enter Release governance for the applicable Release purpose.

Release Admission establishes eligibility to enter Release governance.

Release Candidate formation establishes the identified, integrity-controlled composition evaluated for a defined Release progression.

Release Admission SHALL NOT itself establish:

- Release Candidate Identity;
- Release Candidate composition;
- Release Fingerprint; or
- Candidate Integrity.

The Release System retains authority over Release Candidate semantics.

---

## 20. Release Readiness and Authorization Boundary

Release Admission is distinct from Release Readiness and Release Authorization.

Release Admission SHALL NOT itself establish:

- Release Evidence;
- Release Readiness;
- Release Authorization;
- Release Promotion;
- Release Outcome; or
- Released State.

An admitted Engineering outcome MAY subsequently participate in Release Candidate formation and applicable Release progression under Release System governance.

Eligibility to enter Release governance SHALL NOT be interpreted as readiness or authorization to progress.

---

## 21. Governed Return to Engineering

Release activity MAY identify a need for additional Engineering realization.

Where such a need is identified, Release and applicable collaboration mechanisms MAY preserve:

- discovered Release conditions;
- applicable Release Evidence;
- affected Release Candidate and Release Fingerprint;
- Release impact;
- applicable constraints;
- urgency;
- desired Release need; and
- applicable provenance.

The Engineering System retains authority over the resulting Engineering response, including Engineering solution, planning, Engineering Slices, realization, Engineering Evidence, Engineering Conclusion, and Engineering Completion.

Release or Collaboration activity SHALL NOT silently perform or establish Engineering realization.

---

## 22. Re-entry into Release Governance

A resulting concluded Engineering outcome produced following a governed return to Engineering SHALL enter or re-enter Release governance only through applicable Release Admission.

Prior Release Admission SHALL NOT automatically admit materially new or resulting Engineering outcomes.

The new or resulting Engineering basis SHALL remain distinguishable and traceable.

Following applicable Release Admission, affected Release semantics MAY be reassessed under Release System governance.

---

## 23. Admission Reassessment and Continued Applicability

A prior Release Admission determination SHALL remain applicable only within the material scope and basis for which it was established.

A prior Release Admission determination SHALL NOT be silently applied beyond its:

- Release purpose;
- identified Engineering outcome scope;
- authoritative basis;
- material constraints; or
- other governing conditions relevant to its determination.

Where material changes affect the continued applicability of a prior Release Admission determination, applicable governance SHALL determine whether reassessment or a new Release Admission determination is required.

Reassessment SHALL preserve the prior determination and its provenance rather than silently rewriting history.

---

## 24. Authority Resolution

Applicable Release Admission authority SHALL be established through the participating authority or authorities required by governing policy and context.

This specification does not establish a universal Release Admission role or authority holder.

Authority Resolution MAY determine the authority applicable to a Release Admission interaction.

Authority Resolution SHALL NOT grant, broaden, aggregate, transfer, manufacture, or exercise authority merely through resolution.

The ability to compose, evaluate, recommend, record, approve technically, or execute workflow associated with Release Admission SHALL NOT itself establish authority to make the governed Release Admission determination.

---

## 25. Traceability and Provenance

Release Admission SHALL preserve sufficient traceability and provenance to reconstruct the material path from authoritative Engineering basis to the resulting Release Admission determination.

Applicable traceability SHALL support determination of:

```text
Engineering Conclusion
        ↓
Identified concluded Engineering outcome
        ↓
Authoritative Engineering basis
        ↓
Release Admission interaction
        ↓
Release Admission determination
        ↓
Admitted Release Scope where applicable
        ↓
Release governance
```

Material cross-system inputs SHALL remain traceable to their authoritative sources.

Traceability SHALL NOT imply transfer of semantic ownership.

---

## 26. Preservation and Release Record Relationship

Release Admission SHALL be durably preserved or referenced such that its determination, basis, scope, participating authority or authorities, and provenance remain reconstructable.

This specification does not require a separate canonical Release Admission Record artifact.

The Release Record SHALL preserve the applicable Release Admission determination and admitted Release scope in accordance with Release System governance.

Preservation of Release Admission within or through the Release Record SHALL NOT transfer ownership of the Release Admission collaboration interaction to the Release System.

Recording a Release Admission determination SHALL NOT manufacture that determination or its authority.

---

## 27. Human Participation

Humans MAY participate in Release Admission according to applicable responsibilities, governance, and authority.

Human participation MAY include:

- providing authoritative context;
- resolving ambiguity;
- evaluating applicable inputs;
- identifying constraints;
- determining applicability;
- establishing or contributing to the governed determination;
- reviewing proposed determinations; and
- preserving rationale and provenance.

Human participation SHALL NOT itself establish authority beyond that granted by applicable governance.

---

## 28. AI and Automation Participation

AI and automation MAY participate in Release Admission where permitted by applicable governance.

Participation MAY include:

- resolving applicable Engineering outcomes;
- retrieving Engineering Delivery Records and evidence;
- resolving Release context;
- composing Release Admission context;
- validating completeness;
- identifying inconsistencies;
- evaluating declared conditions;
- preparing proposed determinations;
- recording or preserving provenance;
- reconstructing traceability; and
- surfacing unresolved questions.

Technical capability to compose, evaluate, recommend, validate, record, preserve, or execute Release Admission activity SHALL NOT itself grant authority to establish the Release Admission determination.

Where authority is explicitly delegated to automation, that delegation SHALL be scoped, governed, traceable, and non-self-expanding.

AI or automation SHALL NOT manufacture missing authoritative inputs, participating authority, or cross-system agreement.

---

## 29. Representation Neutrality

Release Admission semantics are independent of any specific representation or implementation technology.

A conforming implementation MAY represent or support Release Admission through:

- source-controlled artifacts;
- structured records;
- workflow systems;
- APIs;
- databases;
- collaboration tools;
- approval mechanisms;
- CI/CD systems;
- Release-management systems;
- automation;
- AI-assisted systems; or
- combinations of these mechanisms.

No particular document, ticket, workflow state, approval form, API, database representation, or tool is required by this specification unless separately established by applicable governance.

Implementation choices SHALL preserve the semantic, authority, traceability, provenance, and conformance obligations established by this specification.

---

## 30. Release Admission Invariants

A conforming Release Admission interaction SHALL preserve the following invariants:

1. Release Admission applies only to identified concluded Engineering outcomes.

2. Release Admission occurs in relation to an established Release and defined Release purpose or governing basis.

3. Release Admission establishes eligibility to enter Release governance and does not establish downstream Release readiness, authorization, progression, outcome, or Released State.

4. Collaboration ownership of Release Admission does not transfer ownership of Product, Engineering, or Release semantics.

5. Participating responsibility or technical capability does not itself establish authority.

6. Release Admission does not redefine Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion.

7. Release Admission does not establish Release Candidate Identity, Release Candidate composition, Release Fingerprint, or Candidate Integrity.

8. Release Admission does not establish or manufacture Capability Acceptance.

9. Additional or resulting Engineering outcomes enter or re-enter Release governance only through applicable Release Admission.

10. A prior Release Admission determination does not silently apply beyond the material scope and basis for which it was established.

11. Material reassessment preserves prior determinations and provenance rather than rewriting history.

12. Material authoritative inputs remain traceable to their authoritative sources.

13. Preservation of Release Admission does not manufacture its determination or authority.

14. AI and automation do not acquire Release Admission authority merely through technical capability.

15. Release Admission semantics remain representation-neutral.

---

## 31. Conformance Requirements

A conforming implementation of Release Admission SHALL:

- preserve Release Admission as a governed Engineering–Release collaboration interaction;
- consume only applicable concluded Engineering outcomes as proposed Engineering inputs to Admission;
- preserve authoritative Engineering and Release semantic ownership;
- maintain a determinable Release Identity and Release purpose or governing basis;
- preserve the material Release Admission basis;
- establish a scoped and reconstructable Release Admission determination;
- distinguish admission from absence of determination and non-admission;
- preserve admitted Release scope where applicable;
- prevent silent admission of additional or resulting Engineering outcomes;
- preserve the distinction between Release Admission and Capability Acceptance;
- preserve the distinction between Release Admission and Release Candidate formation;
- preserve the distinction between Release Admission, Release Readiness, and Release Authorization;
- preserve applicable authority boundaries;
- preserve material traceability and provenance;
- support reassessment without rewriting prior governed history;
- preserve applicable Release Admission through or by reference from the Release Record;
- prevent human, AI, automation, workflow, or tooling capability from manufacturing authority; and
- remain compatible with representation-neutral implementation.

A conforming implementation MAY impose additional project-specific workflow, evidence, participation, approval, or representation requirements provided those requirements do not contradict or weaken this specification.
