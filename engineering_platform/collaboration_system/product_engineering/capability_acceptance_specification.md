# Capability Acceptance Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the governed Product–Engineering collaboration through which an identified realized capability is determined to satisfy or not satisfy the applicable Product-owned intended capability or business intent.

It establishes the semantic boundary, object of acceptance, participation, authoritative inputs, acceptance basis, determination, scope, authority, traceability, provenance, reassessment, and conformance obligations applicable to Capability Acceptance.

Capability Acceptance provides a governed cross-system determination of Product-intent fulfillment without transferring or redefining the authoritative semantics of either the Product System or the Engineering System.

---

## 2. Scope

This specification governs:

- the meaning of Capability Acceptance;
- ownership of the Capability Acceptance collaboration interaction;
- the object of Capability Acceptance;
- participating systems and authorities;
- Capability Acceptance entry conditions;
- authoritative Product and Engineering inputs;
- the Capability Acceptance basis;
- applicable acceptance conditions;
- the Capability Acceptance determination;
- acceptance scope;
- multiple and partially satisfied capability scope;
- the relationship to Engineering Conclusion and Engineering Completion;
- non-acceptance;
- Product change;
- additional Engineering realization;
- reassessment and continued applicability;
- relationships to Release Admission and Release governance;
- authority resolution;
- traceability and provenance;
- preservation of Capability Acceptance;
- human, AI, and automation participation; and
- Capability Acceptance conformance.

This specification does not govern:

- creation or approval of Product intent;
- Product Capability ownership;
- Product planning;
- Engineering planning or realization;
- Engineering Evidence;
- Engineering Conclusion;
- Engineering Completion;
- Release Admission;
- Release Candidate formation;
- Release Readiness;
- Release Authorization;
- Release Promotion;
- Release Outcome;
- Released State; or
- Release Conclusion.

Those semantics remain governed by their applicable authoritative systems and specifications.

---

## 3. Capability Acceptance

Capability Acceptance is the governed Product–Engineering Collaboration determination of whether an identified realized capability satisfies the applicable Product-owned intended capability or business intent.

Capability Acceptance establishes whether Product-intent fulfillment has been demonstrated for the scope and basis to which the determination applies.

Capability Acceptance does not establish:

- Product intent;
- Product Capability;
- Engineering realization;
- Engineering Conclusion;
- Engineering Completion;
- Release Admission;
- Release Candidate formation;
- Release Readiness;
- Release Authorization;
- Release Promotion;
- Release Outcome;
- Released State; or
- Release Conclusion.

Capability Acceptance SHALL remain distinguishable from all such independently governed semantics.

---

## 4. Collaboration Ownership

Capability Acceptance is owned as a governed collaboration interaction by the Collaboration System's Product–Engineering collaboration domain.

The Collaboration System governs the Capability Acceptance interaction and preservation of its resulting determination.

Ownership of the collaboration interaction SHALL NOT transfer ownership of participating systems' authoritative semantics to the Collaboration System.

The Collaboration System SHALL NOT acquire, redefine, broaden, narrow, or exercise Product or Engineering authority merely by governing the Capability Acceptance interaction.

Participating systems retain ownership of their authoritative semantics.

---

## 5. System Boundaries

The Product System establishes and preserves applicable Product truth, including Product intent, Product Capability, Product decisions, Product expectations, and other Product-owned semantics.

The Engineering System establishes and preserves applicable Engineering truth, including Engineering planning, Engineering realization, Engineering Evidence, Engineering Conclusion, Engineering Completion, and the Engineering Delivery Record.

The Collaboration System governs the cross-system interaction through which an identified realized capability is evaluated against applicable Product-owned intent.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT redefine authoritative Product or Engineering semantics.

---

## 6. Object of Capability Acceptance

The object of Capability Acceptance is the identified realized capability relative to the applicable Product-owned intended capability or business intent.

Capability Acceptance evaluates what has been realized against the Product-owned basis applicable to the determination.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT be interpreted as Product acceptance or approval of:

- Engineering planning;
- Engineering architecture;
- Engineering solution choices;
- Engineering Slices;
- implementation techniques;
- Engineering Evidence as Engineering truth;
- Engineering Conclusion;
- Engineering Completion;
- Release Candidate composition;
- Release readiness; or
- Release authorization.

Acceptance of realized Product intent SHALL NOT transfer Product authority over Engineering semantics.

---

## 7. Participating Systems and Authorities

Capability Acceptance requires participation sufficient to establish the applicable cross-system determination under governing authority.

Product participation provides the authoritative Product basis against which the realized capability is evaluated.

Engineering participation provides the authoritative Engineering basis describing the realized capability and applicable Engineering truth.

Other authoritative participants MAY participate where required by applicable governance.

This specification does not prescribe organizational roles, job titles, team structures, stakeholder categories, or universal approval hierarchies.

Responsibility to participate SHALL NOT itself establish authority.

Technical capability to participate SHALL NOT itself establish authority.

---

## 8. Capability Acceptance Entry Conditions

A capability considered for Capability Acceptance SHALL be associated with an applicable Engineering outcome that has reached an Engineering Conclusion sufficient to establish the authoritative realized Engineering basis for the capability being evaluated.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT require universal Epic-level Engineering Completion where the applicable realized capability can be authoritatively identified and evaluated at a narrower governed scope.

Applicable Engineering Completion semantics, Engineering Delivery Records, Engineering Evidence, or other authoritative Engineering semantics MAY contribute to the Engineering basis where relevant.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT manufacture Engineering realization, Engineering Evidence, Engineering Conclusion, or Engineering Completion where those semantics have not been established by the Engineering System.

Where the Product basis or Engineering basis is absent, unresolved, or insufficient for the applicable determination, Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT infer or manufacture the missing authoritative basis.

---

## 9. Capability Acceptance Basis

The Capability Acceptance basis comprises the authoritative Product inputs, authoritative Engineering inputs, applicable acceptance conditions, context, constraints, provenance, and participating authority required to determine whether the identified realized capability satisfies the applicable Product-owned intent.

The Capability Acceptance basis MAY draw upon authoritative inputs from multiple systems or governance domains.

Composition, resolution, presentation, comparison, or evaluation of the Capability Acceptance basis SHALL NOT transfer semantic ownership of its constituent inputs.

The applicable source and authority of material inputs SHALL remain determinable.

---

## 10. Authoritative Product Inputs

Applicable Product inputs MAY include:

- Product Capability;
- applicable Product intent;
- business intent;
- Product expectations;
- applicable Epic Product semantics;
- applicable Product decisions;
- applicable acceptance expectations or conditions;
- Product constraints;
- applicable Product provenance; and
- other Product-owned semantics relevant to Capability Acceptance.

Product retains ownership of these semantics.

Capability Acceptance SHALL consume Product inputs according to their established meaning and SHALL NOT redefine them through the acceptance interaction.

Where authoritative interpretation or clarification of Product-owned intent is required, applicable Product authority SHALL remain determinable.

---

## 11. Authoritative Engineering Inputs

Applicable Engineering inputs MAY include:

- the identified realized capability;
- applicable concluded Engineering outcomes;
- applicable Engineering Conclusion;
- Engineering Completion semantics where relevant;
- applicable Engineering Delivery Record or Records;
- applicable Engineering Evidence;
- relevant Engineering decisions;
- realization constraints;
- applicable provenance; and
- other Engineering-owned semantics relevant to Capability Acceptance.

Engineering retains ownership of these semantics.

Capability Acceptance SHALL consume Engineering inputs according to their established meaning and SHALL NOT reinterpret them as different Engineering conclusions, states, decisions, or realization outcomes.

---

## 12. Other Authoritative Inputs

Capability Acceptance MAY consume other authoritative inputs where required by applicable governance.

Consumption of an authoritative input SHALL NOT transfer ownership or authority over that input to the Collaboration System, Product System, or Engineering System.

The source, applicability, and authority of material cross-system inputs SHALL remain determinable.

---

## 13. Acceptance Conditions

Applicable Capability Acceptance conditions SHALL derive from authoritative Product intent and applicable governance.

Capability Acceptance MAY evaluate explicitly established acceptance expectations, conditions, criteria, measures, constraints, or other Product-owned basis where available.

This specification does not establish a universal Acceptance Criteria artifact, format, checklist, test model, or approval mechanism.

Absence of a universally prescribed Acceptance Criteria artifact SHALL NOT prevent Capability Acceptance where the authoritative Product basis remains sufficient and determinable for the applicable determination.

Applicable acceptance conditions MAY require project-specific forms of evaluation, including functional, experiential, business-rule, measurable, regulatory, or other validation relevant to Product-owned intent.

Such evaluation mechanisms SHALL NOT independently redefine Product intent or Engineering truth.

---

## 14. Capability Acceptance Determination

The Capability Acceptance interaction establishes whether the identified realized capability satisfies the applicable Product-owned intended capability or business intent.

A Capability Acceptance determination MAY establish that the identified realized capability:

- satisfies the applicable Product-owned intent; or
- does not satisfy the applicable Product-owned intent.

These semantic outcomes do not require a universal representation or canonical enumeration.

Where no governed determination has yet been established, absence of a determination SHALL NOT be interpreted as either acceptance or non-acceptance.

A Capability Acceptance determination SHALL preserve a determinable basis sufficient to explain the resulting determination.

---

## 15. Acceptance Scope

A Capability Acceptance determination SHALL be scoped to the material basis for which it was established.

Applicable scope SHALL include or make determinable:

- the identified realized capability;
- applicable Product Capability or Product-owned intent;
- authoritative Product basis;
- authoritative Engineering basis;
- applicable acceptance conditions;
- applicable constraints;
- participating authority or authorities;
- resulting determination;
- material rationale or basis; and
- provenance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT silently apply beyond the material scope for which its determination was established.

---

## 16. Multiple and Partial Capability Scope

A single Capability Acceptance determination MAY evaluate one or more realized capabilities where permitted by applicable governance.

Acceptance of one realized capability SHALL NOT imply acceptance of another capability unless that capability is explicitly within the governed scope of the determination.

Where a determination applies only to a subset of an identified realized capability or Product-owned intent, the accepted and non-accepted scope SHALL remain explicit and distinguishable.

Partial scope SHALL NOT require a universal canonical partial-acceptance state or enumeration.

A scoped determination SHALL NOT be interpreted as acceptance of capability or intent outside that governed scope.

---

## 17. Engineering Conclusion and Engineering Completion Relationship

Capability Acceptance is distinct from Engineering Conclusion and Engineering Completion.

Engineering Conclusion establishes a governed Engineering outcome according to Engineering System semantics.

Engineering Completion establishes applicable Engineering completion semantics according to Engineering System governance.

Capability Acceptance establishes whether an identified realized capability satisfies applicable Product-owned intent.

Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion SHALL NOT itself establish Capability Acceptance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT establish, redefine, override, invalidate, or manufacture Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion.

An Engineering outcome MAY satisfy applicable Engineering completion semantics while the associated realized capability does not satisfy the applicable Product-owned intent.

Such a condition is not itself a contradiction between the Product and Engineering Systems.

---

## 18. Non-Acceptance

Where Capability Acceptance determines that an identified realized capability does not satisfy the applicable Product-owned intent, the determination SHALL preserve the material gap, basis, rationale, affected scope, impact where applicable, and provenance.

Non-acceptance SHALL NOT itself determine that the cause is an Engineering deficiency.

Non-acceptance SHALL NOT itself prescribe or establish the downstream Product or Engineering response.

Applicable governance MAY determine that the appropriate response requires:

- clarification of Product intent;
- Product change;
- additional Engineering realization;
- additional evidence;
- correction or clarification of the acceptance basis;
- reassessment; or
- another applicable governed action.

The cause and resulting response SHALL remain governed by the authoritative system or collaboration domain applicable to that response.

---

## 19. Product Change Relationship

A change to Product-owned intent remains governed by the Product System.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT redefine changed Product intent as though it were the Product basis applicable to prior Engineering realization unless that changed basis has been authoritatively established and made applicable through Product governance.

A capability that satisfied an earlier authoritative Product basis SHALL NOT be represented as an Engineering deficiency solely because Product intent subsequently changed.

Where Product change creates a need for additional or changed Engineering realization, the resulting Product-to-Engineering progression SHALL occur through applicable governed Product, Collaboration, and Engineering mechanisms.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself authorize Engineering realization arising from Product change.

---

## 20. Additional Engineering Realization

A Capability Acceptance determination MAY identify a gap whose governed response requires additional Engineering realization.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself prescribe the Engineering solution, Engineering plan, Engineering Slice structure, realization approach, or Engineering Conclusion.

The Engineering System retains authority over:

- Engineering solution;
- Engineering planning;
- Engineering Slices;
- Engineering realization;
- Engineering Evidence;
- Engineering Conclusion; and
- Engineering Completion.

Where additional Engineering realization produces a new or materially changed realized capability relevant to Product intent, applicable Capability Acceptance MAY subsequently be reassessed.

---

## 21. Reassessment and Continued Applicability

A prior Capability Acceptance determination SHALL remain applicable only within the material scope and basis for which it was established.

A prior determination SHALL NOT be silently applied beyond its:

- realized capability scope;
- Product-owned basis;
- Engineering basis;
- acceptance conditions;
- material constraints; or
- other governing conditions relevant to the determination.

Material change to the realized capability, Product basis, Engineering basis, acceptance conditions, or other applicable basis MAY require Capability Acceptance reassessment.

Applicable governance SHALL determine whether reassessment or a new Capability Acceptance determination is required.

Reassessment SHALL preserve prior determinations, their basis, rationale, and provenance rather than silently rewriting history.

---

## 22. Release Admission Relationship

Capability Acceptance and Release Admission are distinct governed collaboration determinations.

Capability Acceptance determines whether an identified realized capability satisfies applicable Product-owned intent.

Release Admission determines whether one or more concluded Engineering outcomes are eligible to enter Release governance for a defined Release purpose.

Capability Acceptance MAY provide an authoritative input or applicable condition for Release Admission where required by applicable governance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT be universally required before Release Admission unless applicable governance establishes that requirement.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself establish, infer, redefine, or manufacture Release Admission.

Release Admission SHALL NOT itself establish Capability Acceptance.

---

## 23. Release Readiness and Release Authority Boundary

Capability Acceptance MAY provide an authoritative input or applicable condition for Release Readiness where required by Release governance.

Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself establish:

- Release Candidate Identity;
- Release Candidate composition;
- Release Fingerprint;
- Candidate Integrity;
- Release Evidence;
- Release Readiness;
- Release Authorization;
- Release Promotion;
- Release Outcome;
- Released State; or
- Release Conclusion.

Acceptance of a realized capability SHALL NOT be interpreted as readiness, authorization, successful promotion, or Released State.

Release governance retains authority over Release-specific semantics.

---

## 24. Authority Resolution

Applicable Capability Acceptance authority SHALL be established through the participating authority or authorities required by governing policy and context.

This specification does not establish a universal Capability Acceptance role or authority holder.

Authority Resolution MAY determine the authority applicable to a Capability Acceptance interaction.

Authority Resolution SHALL NOT grant, broaden, aggregate, transfer, manufacture, or exercise authority merely through resolution.

Product authority over Product-owned intent and Engineering authority over Engineering-owned truth SHALL remain preserved.

The ability to compose, evaluate, compare, recommend, record, approve technically, or execute workflow associated with Capability Acceptance SHALL NOT itself establish authority to make the governed Capability Acceptance determination.

---

## 25. Traceability and Provenance

Capability Acceptance SHALL preserve sufficient traceability and provenance to reconstruct the material path from authoritative Product and Engineering basis to the resulting Capability Acceptance determination.

Applicable traceability SHALL support determination of:

```text
Authoritative Product intent
        +
Identified realized capability
        +
Authoritative Engineering basis
        ↓
Capability Acceptance interaction
        ↓
Capability Acceptance determination
        ↓
Applicable governed response
```

Material cross-system inputs SHALL remain traceable to their authoritative sources.

Traceability SHALL NOT imply transfer of semantic ownership.

---

## 26. Preservation

Capability Acceptance SHALL be durably preserved or referenced such that its scope, Product basis, Engineering basis, applicable acceptance conditions, determination, participating authority or authorities, material rationale, and provenance remain reconstructable.

This specification does not require a separate canonical Capability Acceptance Record artifact.

A Product, Collaboration, Engineering, Release, or other governed record MAY reference Capability Acceptance where permitted by applicable governance.

Such reference SHALL NOT transfer ownership of the Capability Acceptance collaboration interaction to the referencing system.

Recording or referencing a Capability Acceptance determination SHALL NOT manufacture that determination or its authority.

---

## 27. Human Participation

Humans MAY participate in Capability Acceptance according to applicable responsibilities, governance, and authority.

Human participation MAY include:

- providing authoritative Product or Engineering context;
- resolving ambiguity;
- evaluating applicable inputs;
- evaluating realized capability against Product-owned intent;
- identifying gaps;
- determining applicability;
- establishing or contributing to the governed determination;
- reviewing proposed determinations; and
- preserving rationale and provenance.

Human participation SHALL NOT itself establish authority beyond that granted by applicable governance.

---

## 28. AI and Automation Participation

AI and automation MAY participate in Capability Acceptance where permitted by applicable governance.

Participation MAY include:

- resolving applicable Product intent;
- retrieving Product artifacts;
- resolving the identified realized capability;
- retrieving applicable Engineering records and evidence;
- composing Capability Acceptance context;
- mapping realized capability against Product expectations or conditions;
- evaluating explicitly established acceptance conditions;
- identifying gaps or inconsistencies;
- preparing proposed determinations;
- supporting reassessment;
- recording or preserving provenance;
- reconstructing traceability; and
- surfacing unresolved questions.

Technical capability to compose, compare, evaluate, recommend, validate, record, preserve, or execute Capability Acceptance activity SHALL NOT itself grant authority to establish the Capability Acceptance determination.

Where authority is explicitly delegated to automation, that delegation SHALL be scoped, governed, traceable, and non-self-expanding.

AI or automation SHALL NOT manufacture missing authoritative Product intent, Engineering truth, participating authority, or cross-system agreement.

---

## 29. Representation Neutrality

Capability Acceptance semantics are independent of any specific representation or implementation technology.

A conforming implementation MAY represent or support Capability Acceptance through:

- source-controlled artifacts;
- structured records;
- workflow systems;
- APIs;
- databases;
- collaboration tools;
- approval mechanisms;
- demonstrations;
- validation systems;
- automated tests;
- evidence stores;
- automation;
- AI-assisted systems; or
- combinations of these mechanisms.

No particular acceptance document, UAT artifact, ticket, workflow state, sign-off form, meeting, test suite, approval mechanism, API, database representation, or tool is required by this specification unless separately established by applicable governance.

Implementation choices SHALL preserve the semantic, authority, traceability, provenance, and conformance obligations established by this specification.

---

## 30. Capability Acceptance Invariants

A conforming Capability Acceptance interaction SHALL preserve the following invariants:

1. Capability Acceptance applies to an identified realized capability evaluated against applicable authoritative Product-owned intent.

2. Capability Acceptance establishes Product-intent fulfillment only within its governed scope and basis.

3. Collaboration ownership of Capability Acceptance does not transfer ownership of Product or Engineering semantics.

4. Participating responsibility or technical capability does not itself establish authority.

5. Capability Acceptance does not establish, redefine, override, invalidate, or manufacture Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion.

6. Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion does not itself establish Capability Acceptance.

7. Capability Acceptance does not establish or manufacture Release Admission.

8. Capability Acceptance does not establish Release Candidate semantics, Release Readiness, Release Authorization, Release Promotion, Release Outcome, Released State, or Release Conclusion.

9. Non-acceptance does not itself establish that an Engineering deficiency exists.

10. Product change remains Product-governed and does not become Engineering deficiency merely through Capability Acceptance.

11. Additional Engineering realization remains Engineering-governed.

12. Scoped or partial acceptance does not imply acceptance beyond the governed scope.

13. A prior Capability Acceptance determination does not silently apply beyond the material scope and basis for which it was established.

14. Reassessment preserves prior determinations and provenance rather than rewriting history.

15. Material authoritative inputs remain traceable to their authoritative sources.

16. Preservation of Capability Acceptance does not manufacture its determination or authority.

17. AI and automation do not acquire Capability Acceptance authority merely through technical capability.

18. Capability Acceptance semantics remain representation-neutral.

---

## 31. Conformance Requirements

A conforming implementation of Capability Acceptance SHALL:

- preserve Capability Acceptance as a governed Product–Engineering collaboration interaction;
- evaluate an identified realized capability against applicable authoritative Product-owned intent;
- preserve Product and Engineering semantic ownership;
- maintain a determinable Product basis and Engineering basis;
- preserve applicable acceptance conditions where required;
- establish a scoped and reconstructable Capability Acceptance determination;
- distinguish acceptance from absence of determination and non-acceptance;
- preserve explicit scope where acceptance applies only to part of a capability or Product intent;
- preserve the distinction between Capability Acceptance and Engineering Conclusion or Engineering Completion;
- preserve the distinction between Capability Acceptance and Release Admission;
- preserve the distinction between Capability Acceptance and Release-specific readiness, authorization, progression, outcome, or Released State;
- prevent non-acceptance from automatically manufacturing an Engineering deficiency;
- preserve Product authority over Product change;
- preserve Engineering authority over additional Engineering realization;
- preserve applicable authority boundaries;
- preserve material traceability and provenance;
- support reassessment without rewriting prior governed history;
- preserve Capability Acceptance durably or by governed reference;
- prevent human, AI, automation, workflow, or tooling capability from manufacturing authority; and
- remain compatible with representation-neutral implementation.

A conforming implementation MAY impose additional project-specific workflow, evidence, participation, validation, approval, or representation requirements provided those requirements do not contradict or weaken this specification.
