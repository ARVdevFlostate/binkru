# Epic Refinement

## 1. Purpose

This lifecycle defines the governed collaboration for refining Product-approved Draft Epics into Engineering-ready Epics.

Epic Refinement enables Product and Engineering to collaboratively mature Product-approved Draft Epics by validating business intent, incorporating engineering knowledge, refining Epic boundaries and establishing shared readiness for entry into Engineering planning and governed realization.

The lifecycle preserves approved business intent while refining the Epic into an Engineering-ready state suitable for Engineering planning and governed realization.

Epic Refinement concludes when participating systems agree that the Epic has reached the Engineering-ready state and may enter the Engineering System.

---

# 2. Scope

This lifecycle applies after successful completion of the Engineering Transition lifecycle.

It governs collaboration between the Product System and the Engineering System during collaborative refinement of Product-approved Draft Epics.

---

# 3. Objectives

Epic Refinement aims to:

- validate the business intent of each Product-approved Draft Epic;
- collaboratively refine Product-approved Draft Epics into Engineering-ready Epics;
- preserve approved business intent;
- validate Epic boundaries and scope;
- incorporate engineering knowledge;
- identify assumptions, dependencies and risks;
- identify areas requiring further clarification;
- establish governed agreements throughout refinement;
- establish shared readiness for Engineering planning and governed realization; and
- preserve end-to-end traceability throughout the Epic lifecycle.

---

# 4. Participants

Epic Refinement is a collaborative activity involving representatives from both systems.

Typical participants include:

**Product**

- Product Owner
- Product Manager
- Domain Experts (where appropriate)

**Engineering**

- Engineering Lead
- Solution Architect
- Technical Lead
- Engineering Contributors (where appropriate)

AI assistants may support refinement activities but do not establish governed agreements or assume accountability.

---

# 5. Entry Criteria

Epic Refinement may begin when:

- Engineering Transition has been successfully completed;
- Product-approved Draft Epics are available;
- governed agreements established during Engineering Transition have been recorded;
- supporting Product artifacts are available; and
- participating representatives have been identified.

---

# 6. Refinement Activities

Typical Epic Refinement activities include:

1. Review the Product-approved Draft Epic.
2. Validate the associated business intent.
3. Confirm expected business outcomes.
4. Review Engineering Transition observations.
5. Review assumptions.
6. Refine Epic boundaries.
7. Merge, split or remove Epics where appropriate.
8. Incorporate engineering knowledge.
9. Identify engineering considerations.
10. Identify dependencies.
11. Identify significant risks.
12. Identify areas requiring further clarification.
13. Record Epic refinement observations.
14. Establish governed agreements.
15. Determine readiness to enter the Engineering System for Engineering planning and governed realization.
16. Record the Epic Refinement outcome.

Epic Refinement focuses on collaboratively maturing Product-approved Draft Epics into Engineering-ready Epics.

It does not perform Engineering planning, Engineering realization planning, or implementation planning.

---

# 7. Exit Criteria

Epic Refinement is complete when:

- the Product-approved Draft Epic has been collaboratively refined;
- approved business intent has been preserved;
- Epic boundaries have been agreed;
- engineering considerations have been incorporated;
- dependencies and risks have been documented;
- governed agreements have been recorded;
- required follow-up actions have been identified;
- shared readiness has been established;
- the Epic Refinement Record has been completed; and
- the Epic has reached the Engineering-ready state.

---

# 8. Outputs

Epic Refinement produces:

- an Epic Refinement Record;
- Engineering-ready Epics;
- engineering observations;
- identified assumptions;
- identified dependencies;
- identified risks;
- governed agreements;
- agreed follow-up actions; and
- readiness for Engineering planning and governed realization.

---

# 9. Responsibilities

## Product

Product is responsible for:

- clarifying Product-approved Draft Epics;
- preserving approved business intent;
- validating expected business outcomes;
- participating in collaborative refinement; and
- participating in governed agreements.

---

## Engineering

Engineering is responsible for:

- validating engineering readiness of Epics;
- identifying engineering considerations;
- recommending refinement of Epic boundaries;
- identifying dependencies and risks;
- participating in governed agreements; and
- preparing Engineering-ready Epics to enter the Engineering System.

---

## Shared Responsibilities

Product and Engineering jointly:

- preserve traceability across the Epic lifecycle;
- collaboratively refine Product-approved Draft Epics;
- preserve approved business intent;
- establish governed agreements;
- identify required follow-up actions; and
- determine readiness to enter the Engineering System.

---

# 10. Relationship to Other Lifecycles

Epic Refinement follows successful completion of Engineering Transition.

Successful completion of Epic Refinement transitions Product-approved Draft Epics into the Engineering-ready state.

Engineering-ready Epics become the primary input to the Engineering System for Engineering planning and governed realization.

Epic Refinement does not authorise implementation.

Epic Refinement does not authorise Engineering realization. Authorization and progression of Engineering realization are governed entirely by the Engineering System.

---

# 11. Human and AI Collaboration

Humans remain responsible for:

- refining Epics;
- establishing governed agreements;
- agreeing collaborative outcomes; and
- accepting accountability.

AI may assist by:

- analysing Epics;
- identifying ambiguities;
- recommending Epic refinements;
- suggesting merges or splits;
- identifying dependencies;
- analysing traceability;
- preparing refinement records; and
- recommending areas requiring clarification.

AI does not establish governed agreements or assume accountability.

---

# 12. Conformance

An Epic Refinement conforms to this lifecycle when:

- entry criteria have been satisfied;
- required participants have been involved;
- refinement activities have been completed;
- approved business intent has been preserved;
- governed agreements have been recorded;
- exit criteria have been satisfied;
- traceability has been preserved; and
- the Epic has reached the Engineering-ready state.
