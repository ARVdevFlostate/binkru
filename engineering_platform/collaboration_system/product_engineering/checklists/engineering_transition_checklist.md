# Engineering Transition Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist assists participants in evaluating whether an Engineering Transition has been completed in accordance with the Collaboration Principles and the Engineering Transition lifecycle.

It supports consistent collaboration before progressing to Epic Refinement.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all Engineering Transition activities governed by the Collaboration System.

---

# 3. Review Checklist

## Transition Context

- [ ] An Engineering Transition Record has been created.
- [ ] Product-approved Draft Epic(s) have been identified.
- [ ] Product information has been completed.
- [ ] Supporting Product context has been completed.
- [ ] Product and Engineering representatives have been identified.

---

## Shared Understanding

- [ ] The business intent has been communicated.
- [ ] Product-approved Draft Epics have been reviewed.
- [ ] Expected business outcomes have been understood.
- [ ] Product priorities have been discussed.
- [ ] Significant assumptions have been identified.

---

## Engineering Considerations

- [ ] Engineering observations have been documented.
- [ ] Technical considerations have been identified.
- [ ] Significant dependencies have been identified.
- [ ] Significant engineering risks have been identified.
- [ ] Open questions have been documented.

---

## Collaboration

- [ ] Product representatives actively participated.
- [ ] Engineering representatives actively participated.
- [ ] Significant concerns have been discussed.
- [ ] Shared understanding has been established.
- [ ] Governed Agreements have been recorded.
- [ ] Required follow-up actions have been identified.

---

## Traceability

- [ ] Product-approved Draft Epics have been referenced.
- [ ] Supporting Product artifacts have been referenced.
- [ ] End-to-end traceability has been preserved.
- [ ] Related Collaboration records have been identified where applicable.

---

## Readiness

- [ ] Entry criteria have been satisfied.
- [ ] Exit criteria have been satisfied.
- [ ] The Engineering Transition outcome has been recorded.
- [ ] Product has acknowledged the Engineering Transition outcome.
- [ ] Engineering has acknowledged the Engineering Transition outcome.
- [ ] The collaboration is ready to proceed to Epic Refinement.

---

## Governance

- [ ] Collaboration followed the Collaboration Principles.
- [ ] System ownership has been preserved.
- [ ] Responsibilities have been clearly established.
- [ ] Human accountability has been maintained.
- [ ] Governed Agreements have been recorded.
- [ ] The Engineering Transition Record is complete.

---

# 4. Review Outcome

| Result | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Complete | |
| Complete with Follow-up Actions | |
| Further Collaboration Required | |

Document observations, recommendations or follow-up actions identified during the review.
