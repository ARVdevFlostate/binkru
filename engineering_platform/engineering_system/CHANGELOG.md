# Engineering System Changelog

This changelog records material changes to the canonical Engineering System.

Changes are recorded from the first formally versioned baseline onward.
Development history preceding `1.0.0` is not reconstructed as versioned releases.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-11 — Initial Canonical Baseline

### Established

- Canonical Engineering lifecycle from Engineering-ready Epic through governed Engineering Conclusion and finalized Engineering Delivery Record.
- Engineering Delivery Proposal, Investment Decision, and Approved Investment Baseline semantics.
- Engineering Delivery Planning, Execution Readiness Decision, and Execution Baseline semantics.
- Engineering Slice as the canonical realization unit.
- Engineering Orchestration as the continuous, adaptive realization process.
- Engineering Artifact Model with stable identity and explicit governed relationships.
- Engineering Governance model with explicit, scoped, and non-transitive authority.
- Architecture Decision governance and Architecture Decision Record lifecycle.
- Engineering Delivery Record semantics for progressive realization history and governed Engineering conclusion.
- Engineering Evidence semantics for traceable support of Engineering claims.
- Engineering Composition semantics for controlled derivation of Engineering artifacts.
- Canonical distinction between specifications, templates, and checklists.
- Proportional governance based on Engineering significance and consequence.
- Historical-integrity requirements for governed Engineering records.
- Human, AI, automated, and mixed-team participation model.
- Explicit separation between capability and governance authority.
- Tool-independent canonical semantics suitable for multiple conforming implementations.
- Explicit Engineering System boundary before downstream Release governance.

### Repository

- Established canonical governance, delivery, composition, template, checklist, and technology-standard organization.
- Established canonical template families for Engineering Delivery Proposal, Engineering Delivery Plan, Architecture Decision Record, and Engineering Delivery Record.
- Established independent validation checklists for Proposal, Planning, Orchestration, Architecture Decision Records, Engineering Delivery Records, and Composition.
- Established the root Engineering System documentation and repository navigation model.
